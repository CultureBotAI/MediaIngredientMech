## 2. Native Schema Contract & Prerequisite Gate Audit (`add-uniprot-protein-examples`)

### 2.1 Verified Native Schema Representation (`src/mediaingredientmech/schema/mediaingredientmech.yaml`)

Per `docs/CAUSAL_GRAPH_CONTRACT.md` Sections 2, 3, and 6 and `src/mediaingredientmech/schema/mediaingredientmech.yaml` (lines 1110–1392), MediaIngredientMech represents organism-specific UniProt proteins **exclusively** as `ProteinExample` entries nested under `CausalNode.protein_examples` on `GENE_OR_PROTEIN` or `MOLECULAR_FUNCTION` nodes inside `IngredientRecord.causal_graphs`:

1. **Two-tier taxon-neutral vs. organism-specific separation (`docs/CAUSAL_GRAPH_CONTRACT.md` §1 Decision 5, §2 lines 216–220, §3 lines 263–284):**
   - `CausalNode.grounding` on a `GENE_OR_PROTEIN` node **must** be a taxon-neutral family/ortholog identifier matching `^(?:PR|KOfam|PFAM|NCBIfam|TCDB|InterPro):[A-Za-z0-9_.-]+$` (enforced by closed-schema rule lines 1138–1144 and verified by `TestNodeGroundingConstraint`: `N5` rejects `UniProtKB:P15723` as `CausalNode.grounding`, and `N10` rejects `NCBITaxon:83333` as `CausalNode.grounding`).
   - `CausalNode.grounding` on a `MOLECULAR_FUNCTION` node **must** be an activity identifier matching `^(?:GO:\d{7}|EC:\d+(?:\.(?:\d+|-)){3}|RHEA:\d+|TCDB:\d+\.[A-Z]\.\d+\.\d+\.\d+)$` (lines 1145–1151).
   - Organism-specific protein exemplars live in `CausalNode.protein_examples` (`range: ProteinExample`, `multivalued: true`, `inlined_as_list: true`), which is permitted **only** on `GENE_OR_PROTEIN` and `MOLECULAR_FUNCTION` nodes (`PROTEIN_EXAMPLE_NODE_TYPE` invariant, `docs/CAUSAL_GRAPH_CONTRACT.md` §7 line 514).
2. **`ProteinExample` Closed-Schema Slots & Constraints (`src/mediaingredientmech/schema/mediaingredientmech.yaml` lines 1344–1392):**
   - `uniprot_id` (`required: true`): UniProtKB primary accession CURIE matching `^UniProtKB:(?:[OPQ][0-9][A-Z0-9]{3}[0-9]|[A-NR-Z][0-9](?:[A-Z][A-Z0-9]{2}[0-9]){1,2})$` (6- or 10-character canonical accession; isoform suffixes like `UniProtKB:P09833-2` are rejected by `TestProteinExample::test_protein_example_rejects_bad_uniprot_id`).
   - `protein_label` (`required: true`): Official UniProt recommended protein name (`proteinDescription.recommendedName.fullName.value`, or `submissionNames[0].fullName.value` for unreviewed TrEMBL entries).
   - `gene_symbol` (`required: false`): Primary gene symbol (`genes[0].geneName.value` or `orderedLocusNames[0].value`).
   - `taxon_id` (`required: true`): Organism NCBITaxon CURIE matching `^NCBITaxon:\d+$` (`organism.taxonId`).
   - `taxon_label` (`required: true`): Exact scientific name (`organism.scientificName`).
   - `entry_status` (`required: true`, `range: UniProtEntryStatusEnum`): `REVIEWED` (Swiss-Prot) or `UNREVIEWED` (TrEMBL). Swiss-Prot (`REVIEWED`) is strongly preferred; `UNREVIEWED` is permitted only when no reviewed entry exists for the relevant clade (`docs/CAUSAL_GRAPH_CONTRACT.md` §3 line 284).
   - `proteome_id` (`required: false`): UniProt reference proteome ID matching `^UP\d{9}$`.
   - `retrieved_on` (`required: true`): ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` date (`^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$`) when the UniProt record was fetched and cached in `references_cache/UniProtKB_<acc>.json`.
   - `role` (`required: true`): Free-text functional role of the protein in the causal step (e.g., `"periplasmic molybdate-binding subunit"`, `"vitamin B12 outer-membrane TonB-dependent receptor"`).
   - `evidence` (`required: true`, `multivalued: true`, `minimum_cardinality: 1`, `range: EvidenceItem`): Reference-backed evidence (`PMID:...`, `DOI:...`, or `UniProtKB:...` with `supports`, `evidence_source`, `snippet`, `explanation`).
