## 2. Native Representation & Prerequisite Gate Audit

### 2.1 Native LinkML Representation for `ProteinExample`

In MediaIngredientMech, organism-specific UniProtKB annotations are **not** stored as top-level ingredient fields or inside `CausalNode.grounding`. Per `docs/CAUSAL_GRAPH_CONTRACT.md` Section 1 (Decision 8) and `src/mediaingredientmech/schema/mediaingredientmech.yaml` (lines 714–966):
- **`CausalNode.grounding` must remain organism-neutral** (`EC:*`, `RHEA:*`, `GO:*`, `TCDB:*`, `KO:*`, `PFAM:*`, `NCBIfam:*`, `TIGRFAM:*`, `InterPro:*`, `UBERON:*`). Using `UniProtKB:*` or `NCBITaxon:*` in `CausalNode.grounding` is explicitly rejected by closed-schema rule cases `N5` (`UniProtKB:P09833` in `grounding`) and `N10` (`NCBITaxon:83333` in `grounding`) in `tests/test_causal_graph_schema.py`.
- **`CausalNode.protein_examples`** (`multivalued: true`, `range: ProteinExample`) attaches concrete UniProtKB proteins to `GENE_OR_PROTEIN` or `MOLECULAR_FUNCTION` nodes only (`PROTEIN_EXAMPLE_NODE_TYPE` invariant).
- Each **`ProteinExample`** instance requires:
  - `uniprot_id` (`required: true`, pattern `^UniProtKB:(?:[OPQ][0-9][A-Z0-9]{3}[0-9]|[A-NR-Z][0-9](?:[A-Z][A-Z0-9]{2}[0-9]){1,2})$` — canonical 6- or 10-character accession without isoform suffix).
  - `protein_label` (`required: true`, `recommendedName.fullName.value` or `submittedName[0].fullName.value` from `references_cache/UniProtKB_<accession>.json`).
  - `gene_symbol` (`required: false`, primary `genes[0].geneName.value`).
  - `taxon_id` (`required: true`, pattern `^NCBITaxon:[0-9]+$`).
  - `taxon_label` (`required: true`, `organism.scientificName` from cached UniProt JSON).
  - `entry_status` (`required: true`, `UniProtEntryStatusEnum`: `REVIEWED` for Swiss-Prot, `UNREVIEWED` for TrEMBL).
  - `proteome_id` (`required: false`, pattern `^UP[0-9]{9}$`).
  - `retrieved_on` (`required: true`, ISO date `^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$`).
  - `role` (`required: true`, free-text functional role in the step).
  - `evidence` (`required: true`, `multivalued: true`, `minimum_cardinality: 1`, `range: EvidenceItem`).

## 4. Organism, Sequence, Subunit, and Claim-Scope Verification Rules

In accordance with the Stage 3 prompt (*"Preserve exact organism, sequence and claim scope; document inapplicability and representation/writer blockers"*) and `docs/CAUSAL_GRAPH_CONTRACT.md` (Section 1 Decisions 8–9, Section 5, and Section 7), every verified UniProt candidate in Section 5 was audited against five scope constraints:

1. **Organism-neutral node vs. taxon-scoped `ProteinExample` (`Decision 8`):**
   - `CausalNode.grounding` holds the organism-neutral function or family (`EC:*`, `GO:*`, `TCDB:*`, `KO:*`, `NCBIfam:*`, `PFAM:*`).
   - `ProteinExample.uniprot_id` (`UniProtKB:*`) and `ProteinExample.taxon_id` (`NCBITaxon:*`) hold the exact organism-specific protein instance.
2. **Exact strain-level `NCBITaxon:*` and reference `proteome_id` (`PROTEIN_EXAMPLE_TAXON` & Section 5):**
   - Each `ProteinExample.taxon_id` and `taxon_label` must match the UniProt entry's `organism.taxonId` and `organism.scientificName` (e.g., `NCBITaxon:83333` `Escherichia coli (strain K12)` with `UP000000625`, `NCBITaxon:224308` `Bacillus subtilis (strain 168)` with `UP000001570`, `NCBITaxon:1097` `Chlorobaculum tepidum (strain ATCC 49652 / DSM 12025 / NBRC 103806 / TLS)` with `UP000002697`, `NCBITaxon:1148` `Synechocystis sp. (strain PCC 6803 / Kazusa)` with `UP000001425`, `NCBITaxon:1309` `Streptococcus mutans` for `UniProtKB:P12655` `DltA`).
3. **Canonical sequence vs. isoform/propeptide/mature-chain scope (`ProteinExample.uniprot_id` regex):**
   - `ProteinExample.uniprot_id` enforces the canonical accession regex `^UniProtKB:(?:[OPQ][0-9][A-Z0-9]{3}[0-9]|[A-NR-Z][0-9](?:[A-Z][A-Z0-9]{2}[0-9]){1,2})$` (rejecting isoform suffixes such as `UniProtKB:P09833-2`, verified by test case `PE2` in `tests/test_causal_graph_schema.py`). Any periplasmic signal-peptide cleavage or cofactor covalent attachment (e.g., `ModA` signal peptide 1–24 vs. mature chain 25–257; `SoxB` twin-arginine signal peptide) is noted in `ProteinExample.role` / `evidence.notes` while preserving the canonical accession.
4. **Subunit vs. multi-subunit complex scope:**
   - When an uptake transporter or enzyme is a multi-subunit complex (e.g., `ModABC` molybdate ABC transporter `GO:0015412`, `PtsG`/`Crr` glucose PTS permease `EC:2.7.1.199`, `CysPUWA` sulfate/thiosulfate ABC transporter `GO:0015419`, `SoxYZ`/`SoxAX`/`SoxB` thiosulfate oxidation complex `EC:3.12.1.1`, `LivJHMGF` branched-chain amino acid ABC transporter, or bacterial core RNA polymerase $\alpha_2\beta\beta'\omega$ `EC:2.7.7.6`), `ProteinExample.role` explicitly states the **specific subunit** represented by the `UniProtKB:*` accession (e.g., `ModA` periplasmic substrate-binding protein vs. `ModB` permease vs. `ModC` ATP-binding subunit; `RpoB` $\beta$ subunit containing the rifampicin binding pocket) rather than conflating the single polypeptide with the holocomplex.
5. **Graph-level `scope_taxon_id` vs. node-level `ProteinExample.taxon_id` (`TAXON_SCOPE_MISMATCH` & Scope Gate G4/G5):**
   - If `CausalGraph.scope_taxon_id` is omitted, the graph's biological steps are claimed to be broadly conserved across microbes, and `ProteinExample.taxon_id` identifies the exemplar model organism (`NCBITaxon:83333`, `NCBITaxon:224308`, etc.) supporting that node.
   - If `CausalGraph.scope_taxon_id` is populated (because a pathway is clade-restricted, e.g., green-sulfur/purple-sulfur bacterial `Sox` thiosulfate oxidation or cyanobacterial `CmpABCD` bicarbonate transport), every `ProteinExample.taxon_id` in that graph must be equal to or a taxonomic descendant of `CausalGraph.scope_taxon_id` (`NCBITaxon` `rdfs:subClassOf*` in `mappings/ontology_facts.tsv`), and Scope Gate G4/G5 requires citing a `PEER_REVIEWED_PUBLICATION` demonstrating the taxon-specific mechanism.
