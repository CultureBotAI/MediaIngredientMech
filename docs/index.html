How records are made: MediaIngredientMech is an “LLM-assisted curation system for media ingredient ontology mappings” that “manages the ingredient records aggregated from media recipes in CultureMech” (README). Each record page quotes the schema’s definition of its mapping status and lists its curation events, marking those that record LLM assistance.
