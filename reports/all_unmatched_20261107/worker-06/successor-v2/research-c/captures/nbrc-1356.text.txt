Separately autoclave concentrated Bacto Yeast Extract, coenzyme M, sodium acetate, Na 2 S·9H 2 O and FeS solution under a N 2 atmosphere in tightly closed vessels. Add the autoclaved concentrated solutions, and filter-sterile concentrated MgCl 2 ·6H 2 O, vitamin solution, trace element solution, methanol and sodium formate solution. After re-exchanging with N 2 , add 1 drop of filter-sterile 10% sodium dithionaite solution in 1 M NaHCO 3 by syringe. Final pH of the complete medium should be 9. *Mineral base 1 Na 2 CO 3 185 g NaHCO 3 35 g NaCl 16 g K 2 HPO 4 1 g Distilled water 1 L Mix ingredients and autoclave under a N 2 atmosphere. Because some precipitate appears on the bottom for 1 day after autoclaving, use only supernatant for making the medium. Final pH should be 10. **Mineral base 2 NaCl 240 g NH 4 Cl 0.5 g K 2 HPO 4 2.5 g KCl 5 g Distilled water 1 L Mix ingredients and autoclave under a N 2 atmosphere.
