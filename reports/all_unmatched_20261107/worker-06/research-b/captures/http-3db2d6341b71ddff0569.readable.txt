320. CLOSTRIDIUM CELLULOVORANS MEDIUM


K2HPO4 x 3 H2O                                                     1.00       g
NH4Cl                                                              1.00       g
KCl                                                                0.50       g
MgSO4 x 7 H2O                                                      0.50       g
Cysteine-HCl x H2O                                                 0.15       g
Trypticase peptone                                                 0.50       g
Yeast extract                                                      0.50       g
Cellobiose                                                         5.00       g
Rumen fluid, clarified or sludge fluid (see medium 119)           20.00      ml
Trace element solution SL-10 (see below)                           1.00      ml
Na2CO3                                                             1.00       g
Resazurin                                                          1.00      mg
Na2S x 9 H2O                                                       0.15       g
Distilled water                                                 1000.00      ml



Dissolve ingredients (except cellobiose, bicarbonate and sulfide), boil medium for 1 min.,
then cool to room temperature under 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture. Dispense under
same gas atmosphere in culture vessels and autoclave. Add sulfide from a sterile anoxic
stock solution prepared under N2 and bicarbonate from a sterile anoxic stock solution
prepared under 80% N2 and 20% CO2. Sterilize cellobiose separately by filtration. Adjust
pH of the completed medium to 7.0. Some strains can be adapted to cellulose as
substrate using 10.0 g/l cellulose MN 301 (MACHEREY-NAGEL).



Trace element solution SL-10:
 HCl (25%; 7.7 M)                                                 10.00      ml
 FeCl2 x 4 H2O                                                     1.50       g
 ZnCl2                                                            70.00      mg
 MnCl2 x 4 H2O                                                   100.00      mg
 H3BO3                                                             6.00      mg
 CoCl2 x 6 H2O                                                   190.00      mg
 CuCl2 x 2 H2O                                                     2.00      mg
 NiCl2 x 6 H2O                                                    24.00      mg
 Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O                                                  36.00      mg
 Distilled water                                                 990.00      ml

First dissolve FeCl2 in the HCl, then dilute in water, add and dissolve the other salts.
Finally make up to 1000.0 ml.
