 Trace element solution (see below) |  1.0     | ml |

 NaHCO3 |  2.5     | g | 
 Resazurin |  1.0     | mg | 
 Distilled water |  900.0     | ml | 
Mix components except NaHCO3 and adjust pH to 6.5. Bring to a boil for 5-10 sec, then, cool down under a stream of N2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) and add NaHCO3 to the solution. Dispense 0.9 volume of the medium into culture vessels (e.g., 18 ml of the medium in 50 ml serum bottle) under a stream of N2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. After autoclaving, stand the medium overnight. 
Solution B: 
 Sodium pyruvate |  2.2     | g | 
 Distilled water |  100.0     | ml | 
Adjust pH to 7.0 and filter-sterilize. Keep the solution under a N2 atmosphere. 
To complete the medium, add 0.1 volume of Solution B to the culture vessels containing Solution A. Prior to inoculation, aseptically and anaerobically add 0.01 volume of L-cysteine·HCl·H2O and Na2S·9H2O solutions to the medium (3% solutions, autoclaved and stored under a N2 atmosphere). 
Comment: For strain JCM 10971, incubate with a 10% inoculum.
