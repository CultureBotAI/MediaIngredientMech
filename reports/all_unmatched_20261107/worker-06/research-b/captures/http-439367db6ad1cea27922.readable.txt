Prepare the medium anoxically under 80% H2 + 20% CO2 gas atmosphere. For
incubation use the same gas mixture at two atmospheres of pressure. If the medium is
being used without gas mixture overpressure then adjust pH with a little hydrochloric
acid. Final pH should be 7.5 for strain DSM 15219, DSM 16458 and DSM 18860; 7.0 for
strains DSM 1497, DSM 1537, DSM 2067, DSM 2279, DSM 2373, DSM 17251 and DSM
17508; 6.8 for strain DSM 2095; and 6.5 for strain DSM 1498, DSM 15558 and DSM
22353.

For DSM 2373 increase the amount of trypticase to 6 g/l.

For DSM 2831 prepare the medium under 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas atmosphere. After
sterilization add 5.0 g/l sucrose from a sterile, anoxic stock solution prepared under N2.
The final pH should be 7.0.

For DSM 4254 add a filter-sterilized, anoxic solution of L-histidine to a final concentration
of 80 mg/l.

For DSM 14042 prepare the medium under 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas atmosphere. After
sterilization add 0.05% methanol from a sterile, anoxic stock solution prepared under N 2.
For incubation use the same gas mixture at two atmospheres of pressure. The final pH
should be 7.0.

For DSM 15558 and DSM 16458 supplement medium after autoclaving with 0.5 g/l
coenzyme M (mercaptoethanesulfonic acid) added from a filter-sterilized anoxic stock
solution prepared under N2. Use only one atmosphere overpressure of 80% H2 and CO2.

For DSM 21626 reduce the amount of NaCl to 6 g/l.
