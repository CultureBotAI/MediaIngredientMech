Mix components, adjust pH to 7.2, bring to a boil for a few seconds and cool down under a stream of a N 2 -CO 2 (80:20) gas mixture. Distribute Solution A into culture vessels (e.g., 9 ml per tube) under the same gas mixture, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave. 
To complete the medium, add 0.1 volume of 3.5% NaHCO 3 solution [filter-sterilized and stored under a N 2 -CO 2 (80:20) gas mixture] and reduce the medium with 0.3 g/L (final) L-cysteine·HCl·H 2 O and 0.3 g/L (final) Na 2 S·9H 2 O (autoclave and store 3% stock solutions under a N 2 atmosphere).
