Mix components thoroughly, except NaHCO 3 , and adjust pH to 6.5. Bring to a boil for 5-10 sec, then, cool down under a stream of H 2 -CO 2 (80:20, v/v) and add NaHCO 3 to the medium. Dispense the medium into suitable culture vessels (e.g., 5 ml medium in Hungate tubes) under the same gas mixture and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. After autoclaving, stand the medium overnight.
