For vial cultures, dissolve all compounds except Na 2 S·9H 2 O and L-cysteine·HCl·H 2 O, then, adjust pH to 7.5. Dispense 19 ml of medium into 50-ml vials, purge with N 2 -CO 2 (80:20) gas and fix with a stopper. After autoclaving, add filter-sterilized Na 2 S·9H 2 O and L-cysteine·HCl·H 2 O solution to each vial as reducing agents.
