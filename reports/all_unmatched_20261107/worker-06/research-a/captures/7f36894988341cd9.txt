1682. Yeast nitrogen base glucose (YNBG)

Yeast Nitrogen Base                                              6.7       g
Glucose                                                          2.0       g
Yeast extract                                                    0.2       g
Distilled water                                                 1000      mL

Dissolve the ingredients in 500 mL distilled water and adjust pH to 4.0 and autoclave.
Dissolve 20 g agar in 500 mL distilled water and autoclave. Combine both after
autoclavation.
