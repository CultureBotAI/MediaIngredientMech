 HEPES solution: 
 NaOH  12.0     g 
 HEPES (free acid)  119.2     g 
Dissolve NaOH in 300 ml distilled water. While stirring vigorously, slowly add HEPES until completely dissolved. Fill up to 450 ml with distilled water. If 1 ml of this solution is added to 100 ml of the medium (containing all additive but HEPES), pH should be around 7.6 at 30ºC. Otherwise, adjust pH of the HEPES solution with 10 N NaOH or conc. HCl. Finally fill up to 500 ml with distilled water.
