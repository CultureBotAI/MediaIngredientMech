To complete the medium, combine Solutions A, B, C and 1 ml Vitamin solution (see below) aseptically under a stream of a N 2 -CO 2 (4:1, v/v) gas mixture. Distribute the medium into sterilized culture vessels under the same gas mixture and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Then, add 0.01 volume of 5% Na 2 S·9H 2 O solution (autoclaved under a N 2 atmosphere). Check the final pH to be 7.0 - 7.2. Pressurize the inoculated vessels to 70 kPa N 2 -CO 2 (4:1, v/v) overpressure.
