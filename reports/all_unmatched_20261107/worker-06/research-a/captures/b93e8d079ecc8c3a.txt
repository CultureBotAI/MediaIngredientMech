 Medium for Co-culture of Strain JT with Methanospirillum hungatei NBRC 100397 
 Composition 
   Na-terephthalate 0.42 g   Yeast extract 0.2 g   KH 2 PO 4 0.14 g   MgCl 2 ·H 2 O 0.2 g   CaCl 2 ·2H 2 O 0.15 g   NH 4 Cl 0.54 g   Vitamin solution* 3 ml   Trace element solution** 1 ml   Se/W solution*** 1 ml   Resazurin solution**** 1 ml   NaHCO 3 2.5 g   Na 2 S·9H 2 O 0.3 g   Cysteine-HCl 0.3 g   Distilled water 1 L     This medium is for co-culture of strain JT with Methanospirillum hungatei strain NBRC 100397.     For vial cultures, dissolve all ingredients except for Na-terephthalate, Na 2 S·9H 2 O and cysteine-HCl (up to 900ml), flush N 2 /CO 2 (80/20), and autoclave. Add 0.5 ml of each of filter-sterilized Na 2 S·9H 2 O solution (0.6g Na 2 S·9H 2 O/50 ml) and cysteine-HCl solution (0.6g cysteine-HCl/ 50ml) per each vial separately. Add 0.2 ml of neutralized and filter-sterilized 0.2 M Na-terephthalate solution. Yeast extract is also added at final concentration about 0.02%. Inoculum size 10-20%.
