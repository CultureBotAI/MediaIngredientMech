   NaCl 30 g   K 2 HPO 4 0.14 g   CaCl 2 ·2H 2 O 0.14 g   NH 4 Cl 0.25 g   MgSO 4 ·7H 2 O 3.4 g   MgCl 2 ·6H 2 O 4.2 g   KCl 0.33 g   Fe(NH 4 ) 2 (SO 4 ) 2 ·6H 2 O 0.01 g   Bacto Yeast Extract (Difco) 1 g   Trace elements solution* 10 ml   Vitamin solution** 2 ml   NaHCO 3 2.5 g   Sulfur (powder) 3 g   Resazurin 1 mg   Na 2 S·9H 2 O 0.5 g   Distilled water 1 L     Mix ingredients except for vitamin solution, sulfur, and Na 2 S·9H 2 O. Dispense the medium into suitable culture vessels under a stream of N 2 /CO 2 (80/20), seal with butyl rubber stoppers, and autoclave at 121°C for 15 min. Separately neutralize and autoclave Na 2 S·9H 2 O (5% solution) under N 2 atmosphere. Sterilize vitamin solution by filtration. Sulfur is sterilized separately by autoclaving for 60 min at 105°C on each of 3 successive days. Prior to inoculation, aseptically and anaerobically add vitamin, sulfur, and Na 2 S·9H 2 O.
