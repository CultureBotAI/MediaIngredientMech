Mix ingredients, except NaHCO 3 and Na 2 S·9H 2 O, and bring to a boil for 5-10 sec. Cool down under a stream of N 2 and add NaHCO 3 to the medium. Anaerobically dispense the medium into culture vessels, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave. Separately autoclave 5% Na 2 S·9H 2 O solution under a N 2 atmosphere. Prior to inoculation, aseptically and anaerobically add the Na 2 S·9H 2 O solution to the medium. Check final pH to be 7.2-7.4.
