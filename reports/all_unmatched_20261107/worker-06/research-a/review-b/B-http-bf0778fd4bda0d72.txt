Prepare the medium anoxically under 80% N2 + 20% CO2 gas mixture. Add phosphates
and bicarbonate to the medium after autoclaving from sterile stock solutions autoclaved
under N2 (phosphates) or 80% N2 and 20% CO2 (bicarbonate) gas atmosphere. Adjust pH
of completed medium to pH 7.0.

For DSM 3132 prepare the medium without betaine; add fructose at 0.5% final
concentration from a filter-sterilized anoxic stock solution.
For DSM 4440 use 1.35 g/l betaine; cysteine and sodium sulfide must be replaced by 1
mmol/l dithiothreitol (DTT).
For DSM 16652 replace betaine with 1.11 g/l N-acetyl-D-glucosamine added to the
autoclaved medium from a filter-sterilized anoxic stock solution.
