1. Dissolve ingredients of solution A, adjust pH to 5.0 with 0.1 N HCl, and sparge with 80%
H2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture for 30 - 45 min to make it anoxic. Dispense solution A under
the same gas atmosphere into anoxic serum vials (20 ml medium in 100 ml bottles) and
autoclave. Solutions B to F are prepared under a 100% N2 gas atmosphere and sterilized
by filtration. Before inoculation, complete the medium by adding the appropriate amounts
of solutions B to F to sterile solution A in the sequence indicated.
2. Adjust pH of the complete medium to 5.1 with a sterile anoxic solution of 1 N HCl. After
inoculation pressurize vials to 1 bar overpressure with sterile 80% H2 and 20% CO2 gas
mixture.
3. Note: Use 10-20% (v/v) as inoculum.
