 Medium Detail Information 
 
 
 
 
 Medium No. 
 
 406 
 
 
 
 Medium 
 
 Methanobacterium Medium 
 
 
 
 Composition 
 
 
 
   KH 2 PO 4 0.3 g   K 2 HPO 4 0.3 g   (NH 4 ) 2 SO 4 0.3 g   NaCl 0.6 g   MgSO 2 ·7H 2 O 0.13 g   FeSO 2 ·7H 2 O 2 mg   CaCl 2 ·2H 2 O 8 mg   Bacto Yeast Extract (Difco) 2 g   Trypticase Peptone (BBL) 2 g   Sodium acetate 2.5 g   Sodium formate 2.5 g   Trace minerals* 10 ml   Trace vitamins** 10 ml   NaHCO 3 5 g   Na 2 S·9H 2 O 0.5 g   Cysteine-HCl 0.5 g   Resazurin 1 mg   Distilled water 1 L     Mix ingredients except NaHCO 3 , cysteine-HCl and Na 2 S·9H 2 O, and bring to a boil for 5-10 sec. Cool down under a stream of H 2 /CO 2 (80/20), and add NaHCO 3 to the medium. Dispense the medium into suitable culture vessels (e.g. 20 ml of the medium in 120 ml serum bottles) under a stream of H 2 /CO 2 (80/20) and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Separately autoclave cysteine-HCl and Na 2 S·9H 2 O as 5% solutions, respectively, under a N 2 atmosphere. Prior to inoculation, aseptically and anaerobically add the cysteine-HCl and Na 2 S·9H 2 O solutions. Pressurize the inoculated vessels to 200 kPa with H 2 /CO 2 (80/20).                   *Trace minerals       Nitrilotriacetic acid 1.5 g       MgSO 4 ·7H 2 O 3 g       MnSO 4 ·xH 2 O 0.5 g       NaCl 1 g       FeSO 4 ·7H 2 O 0.1 g       CoSO 4 ·7H 2 0 0.1 g       CaCl 2 ·2H 2 O 0.1 g       ZnSO 4 ·7H 2 O 0.1 g       CuSO 4 ·5H 2 O 0.01 g       AlK(SO 4 ) 2 0.01 g       H 3 BO 3 0.01 g       Na 2 MoO 4 ·2H 2 O 0.01 g       Distilled water 1 L     Dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH solution. Then proceed to add minerals. Adjust final pH to 7.0.                   **Trace vitamins       Biotin 2 mg       Folic acid 2 mg       Pyridoxine-HCl 10 mg       Thiamine-HCl 5 mg       Riboflavin 5 mg       Nicotinic acid 5 mg       Calcium pantothenate 5 mg       Vitamin B 12 0.1 mg       p -Aminobenzoic acid 5 mg       Lipoic acid 5 mg       Distilled water 1 L
