Mix components thoroughly. For preparation of solid medium, add 15.0 g/L agarose. Autoclave and, after cooling to 60C, add the following solutions (autoclaved or *filter-sterilized): 
 
 0.1 M KH 2 PO 4 solution  1.0     ml 
 Trace vitamins* (see Medium No. 197 )  10.0     ml 
 Vitamin B 12 solution* (see Medium No. 403 )  4.0     ml 
 1.0 mM CeCl 3 solution*  1.0     ml 
 1.0 N H 2 SO 4 solution*  4.0     ml 
 
Check pH to be 2.0 to 3.0. Cultivate with methane or methanol as a substrate as described below: 
Methane. Aseptically distribute the medium into sterile serum bottles (leave ≥ 80% gas phase by volume) and seal with rubber stoppers. Add sterile CO 2 and methane (respectively 2-10% and 5-20% by volume) in the gas phase. Cultivate with shaking. 
Methanol: Aseptically distribute the medium into culture vessels (e.g., 10 ml in 60 ml serum bottles), supplement with 0.4-1.0 ml per liter methanol (filter-sterilized) and seal with rubber stoppers. Add sterile CO 2 (2-10% by volume) in the gas phase.
