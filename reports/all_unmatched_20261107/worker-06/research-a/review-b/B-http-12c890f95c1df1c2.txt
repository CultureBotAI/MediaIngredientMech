Mix ingredients, except Na 2 S·9H 2 O, and adjust pH to 6.5 with HCl. Distribute the medium into culture vessels (e.g., 20 ml of the medium in 120 ml serum bottles) under a stream of H 2 -CO 2 (4:1, v/v) gas mixture and seal with butyl rubber stoppers, and autoclave. Neutralize Na 2 S·9H 2 O as a 5% solution, and autoclave under a N 2 atmosphere. Add the sterile Na 2 S solution to the medium, and pressurize the bottles to 200 kPa H 2 -CO 2 (4:1, v/v). After incubation at 70C for 1 hr, readjust pH to 6.5 with a sterile and anaerobic NaHCO 3 stock solution.
