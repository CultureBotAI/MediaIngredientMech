Mix ingredients except KP buffer, vitamin solution, Na2CO3 and Na2S·9H2O. Dispense the medium into suitable culture vessels under a stream of H2/CO2 (80/20) and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Separately autoclave KP buffer and Na2S·9H2O (5% solution) under a N2 atmosphere. Sterilize vitamin and 10% Na2CO3 solutions by filtration. Prior to inoculation, aseptically and anaerobically add KP buffer, vitamin, Na2CO3 and Na2S·9H2O solutions. Pressurize the inoculated vessels to 150 kPa with H2/CO2 (80/20).
