## Substantive challenges

- Does NCITC1295 denote a class rather than M1? Closed by full ontology definition, exact synonym and CAS: it denotes VirginiamycinM1 despite preferred label StreptograminA.
- Do AburamycinA/CinerubinA promotions add unsupported congener choice? Closed by independently re-opened supplier sheets and exact target labels/formulas; A2 and letterA explicitly stated.
- Does Angustmycin candidate silently retain wrong mapped placement or restore C identity? Closed by741 curator ruling and explicit proposal obligations for relocation/no row; original ambiguous name and raw/rejected provenance retained.
- Are bis assay labels safely normalizable to mono-nitrophenyl ChEBI terms? No: fresh BacDive and full ChEBI structures confirm contradiction. Existing unresolved decisions are correct; root notified for deduplicated tracking issue.
