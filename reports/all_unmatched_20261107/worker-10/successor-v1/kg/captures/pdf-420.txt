420. BLOOD AGAR I

Sterilize blood agar No 2 (Oxoid). After autoclaving cool to 50C, then add 40 ml/l of
sterile horse blood.
Rehydrate pellets of freeze-dried ampoules in liquid medium 220 or 1. For rehydration of
Helicobacter and Campylobacter spp., use medium 215 (brain heart infusion).
