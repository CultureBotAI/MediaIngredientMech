301. FALCIVIBRIO MEDIUM

Wilkins-Chalgren-Anaerobe-Broth (Oxoid) is supplemented with 0.3 g/l of L-cysteine and
1.0 mg/l of resazurin. Prepare the medium under 100% nitrogen gas. To the autoclaved
medium add 50 ml/l of sterile serum (horse, bovine or lamb).
