ATCC Medium: 37 Chromatium Medium

Solution 1
CaCl2………………………………………0.8 g
DI Water…………………………………...1000 ml

Adjust the pH to 6.6-6.8. The medium should be dispensed into sterile 16x125
screw cap tubes. Fill the tubes to about 1/8 of an inch from the top.
Autoclave at 121ºC
Note: Media may be dispensed aseptically to avoid boil over. If not, I would
recommend using rubber screw caps.

Solution 2
Heavy Metal Solution (see below)…………….50.0 ml
Vitamin B12 Solution (see below)……………...3.0 ml
Vitamin Solution (see below)………………….15.0 ml
KH2PO4…………………………………………..1.0 g
KCl………………………………………………..1.0 g
NH4Cl……………………….…………………….0.8 g
MgCl2 x 6H2O……………………………………0.8 g
Sodium Ascorbate……………………………....2.4 g
DI Water……………………………….………….32.0 ml

Solution 3
NaHCO3…………………………………….…….4.5 g
DI Water……………………………….………….900.0 ml

Combine Solutions 2 and 3. Adjust the pH to 6.6-6.8. The medium should be
dispensed into sterile 16x125 screw cap tubes. Fill the tubes to about 1/8 of an
inch from the top.
Autoclave @ 121C.

Note: Media may be dispensed aseptically to avoid boil over. If not, I would
recommend using rubber screw caps.
