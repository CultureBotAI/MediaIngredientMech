ATCC Medium 1395: Middlebrook 7H9 Broth w/ ADC Enrichment

Base Medium
Middlebrook 7H9 Broth…………………………….. 4.7 g
Glycerol………………………………………………. 2.0 mL
DI Water……………………………………………… 900.0 mL
ADC Enrichment (see below) …………………….. 100.0 mL

Add 4.7 grams of dehydrated Middlebrook 7H9 medium to 900 mL of water
containing 2.0 mL of glycerol.

Mix well and autoclave at 121ºC. Cool to 50-55ºC.

Aseptically add filter sterilized ADC Enrichment.

ADC Enrichment
Bovine Serum Albumin, Frac V……………………. 5.0 g
Dextrose……………………………………………… 2.0 g
Catalase(beef)………………………………………. 3.0 mg
DI Water…………………………………………….. 100.0 mL

Filter sterilize.
