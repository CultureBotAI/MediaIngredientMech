 946   KORTHOF'S MEDIUM 
 
 10 x Korthof's basal medium (see below)  50.0     ml 
 Rabbit serum heat-inactivated at 56℃ for 30 min  50.0     ml 
 Distilled water  400.0     ml 
 
Autoclave distilled water. After autoclaving, allow to cool, aseptically add 10 x Korthof's basal medium and heat-inactivated rabbit serum that has been filter-sterilized ahead of time. 
 10 x Korthof's basal medium: 
 
 NaCl  7.0     g 
 Na 2 HPO 4  4.4     g 
 Polypeptone peptone (BD-BBL)  4.0     g 
 KH 2 PO 4  1.2     g 
 NaHCO 3  0.1     g 
 KCl  0.2     g 
 CaCl 2 ·2H 2 O  0.2     g 
 Distilled water  450.0     ml 
 
Mix ingredients except CaCl 2 and autoclave. Dissolve CaCl 2 in 50 ml distilled water and filter-sterilize. After autoclaving, allow to cool, add sterile CaCl 2 solution to the medium.
