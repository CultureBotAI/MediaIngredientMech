 MC II (Modified Chang's Medium (MC))
 Freshwater protozoa



 Medium per litre
 Casein digest (Casitone, cat. no. 225930) * 10.0 g
 Na2HPO4 anydrous 1.325 g
 KH2PO4 0.8 g
 Yeast extract (Oxoid LP0021) ** 5.0 g
 D­glucose 2.5 g

 To be added later:

 Sterile foetal calf serum
 (Gamma­Irradiated, cat. no. 10109­155) *** 5 x 20 ml

 Add the first five constituents in the order shown to 900 ml of deionised water,
 allowing each to dissolve completely before adding the next. Adjust to pH 6.9.
 Dispense into 5 x 180 ml aliquots and sterilise by pressure cooking at 10 psi for 15
 minutes.

 Aseptically add the foetal calf serum to a final concentration of 10%.

 Store at 4°C.
