Medium
 Brain Heart Infusion (BHI) with Sodium Acetate
Composition
  Bacto Brain Heart Infusion (BD Difco) 37 g
  Sodium acetate 2.7 g
  Antifoam solution (only for liquid medium)* 2.5 mL
  Distilled water 1 L
  Agar (if needed) 15 g
  pH unadjusted  
 
    For liquid medium: Dispense the medium into suitable culture vessels and autoclave at 121°C for 15 minutes under a N2 atomosphere.
For agar plate: Sterilizing by autoclaving at 121°C for 15 minutes. After autoclaving, cool to about 50°C, and dispense into sterile petri dishes.
             
    *Antifoam solution
      Antifoam SI (Wako) 1 mL
