How do I heat-inactivate serum?
Heat it at 56°C in a water bath for 30 minutes and swirl the
bottle every 10 minutes or so. For accuracy, use a second
bottle of similar size as a control and add an equivalent
volume of water to the control bottle. Place a thermometer
in the control bottle to see when 56°C is reached. Set your
timer for 30 minutes at this point.
