1677: Cyanobacteria Medium BA+

Final volume: 1000 ml


   NaCl (250 g/l stock solution)                             33.30      g
   MgCl2 x 6 H2O (200 g/l stock solution)                     3.30      g
   KCl (50 g/l stock solution)                                3.30      g
   Mg(SO4) x 7 H2O (350 g/l stock solution)                   3.30      g
   CaCl2 x 2 H2O (50 g/l stock solution)                      3.30      g
   Na3-citrate x 2 H2O (0.6 g/l stock solution)               8.30     ml
   Na2-EDTA x 2 H2O (0.1 g/l stock solution)                  8.30     ml
   T-Trace Metal Mix *                                        1.00     ml

1. Make up to 1000 ml with MilliQ water.
2. Autoclave at 121 °C for 20 min.
3. After cooling, add the following filter sterilized components:
   NaNO3 (300 g/l stock solution)                              4.15    ml
   K2HPO4 x 3 H2O (8.0 g/l stock solution)                     4.15    ml
   Na2CO3 (20 g/l stock solution)                              2.30    ml
   Fe(NH4)citrate (6.0 g/l stock solution)                     0.80    ml
   Vitamin B12 (0.02 g/l stock solution)                       1.00    ml




T-Trace Metal Mix *
   ZnSO4 x 7 H2O                                              0.22     g/l
   MnCl2 x 4 H2O                                              1.81     g/l
   H3BO3                                                      2.86     g/l
   Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O                                            0.39     g/l
   CuSO4 x 5 H2O (0.08 g/l stock solution)                   10.00    ml/l
   Co(NO3)2 x 6 H2O (0.05 g/l stock solution)                10.00    ml/l

Make up to 1000 ml with MilliQ water.
