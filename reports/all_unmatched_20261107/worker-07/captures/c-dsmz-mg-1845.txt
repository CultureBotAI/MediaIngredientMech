1845: Nitrososphaera gargensis medium

Final pH: 8.5
Final volume: 1000 ml


   Basal salt stock solution (20x)                         50.00       ml
   Trace element solution (Nitrososphaera)                  1.00       ml
   FeNaEDTA solution (7.5 mM)                               1.00       ml
   Selenite-tungstate solution                              1.00       ml
   NH4Cl (1 M)                                              0.50       ml

1. Add 50 mL of 20x Basal salt stock solution and 4 g CaCO3 to 950 mL sterile double
distilled water and mix all just before use.
2. Autoclave at 121 °C for 20 min.
3. Cool down to room temperature.
4. Add aseptic per liter of 1x basalt salt solution: 1 mL FeNaEDTA (7.5 mM), 1 mL trace
elements solution, 1 mL Selenite-tungstate solution, 0.5 mL NH4Cl solution.
5. Adjust pH to 8.5 with NaOH.
