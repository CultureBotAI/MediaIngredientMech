Medium Detail Information
Medium No.
1116
Medium
Nitrate mineral salts medium (NMS) + 0.01% Tryptone
Composition
MgSO4·7H2O1g
CaCl2·6H2O0.2g
Chelated Iron Solution*2ml
KNO31g
Trace Element Solution**0.5ml
KH2PO40.272g
Na2HPO4·12H2O0.717g
Bacto Tryptone (Difco)0.1g
Purified Agar (e.g., Oxoid L28)12.5g
Distilled deionized water1L
pH 6.8
Dispense the medium into suitable culture vessels, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave at 121°C for 15 min. Prior to inoculation, aseptically add the filter-sterilized methane gas to the vessel. The methane and air ratio in the headspace is about 1:4. Methane should be of high purity.

Ferric ammonium citrate***0.1g
EDTA, sodium salt0.2g
HCl (concentrated)0.3ml

H3BO330mg
CoCl2·6H2O20mg
CaCl2·2H2O1mg
NiCl2·6H2O2mg
Na2MoO4·2H2O3mg
Distilled water1L
Autoclave at 121C for 15 minutes.
***Ferric ammonium citrate
0.05 g of Ferric chloride may be substituted.
