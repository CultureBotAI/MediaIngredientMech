1267 MODIFIED PICROPHILUS MEDIUM
(NH4)2SO4 0.2 g
MgSO4·7H2O 0.5 g
CaCl2·2H2O 0.25 g
Distilled water 1.0 L
Mix components thoroughly, adjust pH to 2.0 - 2.5 with H2SO4 and autoclave. After cooling, add the following solutions (filter-sterilized):
10% Tryptone solution 11.1 ml
10% Yeast extract solution 11.1 ml
10% Fe2(SO4)3 solution (in 0.01 N H2SO4 solution) 88.8 ml
Wolfe's mineral elixir (see Medium No. 470) 2.0 ml
Vitamin solution (see below) 2.0 ml
Vitamin solution:
Vitamin B12 50.0 mg
DL-Calcium pantothenate 50.0 mg
Riboflavin 50.0 mg
Pyridoxine-HCl 10.0 mg
Biotin 20.0 mg
Folic acid 20.0 mg
Nicotinic acid 25.0 mg
Nicotine amide 25.0 mg
Lipoic acid 50.0 mg
p-Aminobenzoic acid 50.0 mg
Thiamine-HCl 50.0 mg
Distilled water 1.0 L
Stir for several hours to dissolve.
