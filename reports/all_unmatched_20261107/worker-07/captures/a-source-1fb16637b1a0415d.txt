579 BACILLUS AA MEDIUM
Solution A:
KH2PO4 0.3 g
K2HPO4 0.3 g
NH4Cl 1.0 g
NaCl 1.0 g
KCl 0.1 g
Yeast extract 1.0 g
Trace minerals (see Medium No. 151) 10.0 ml
Distilled water 1.0 L
Mix ingredients and adjust pH to 7.2. Distribute the solution into tubes (e.g., 10 ml in the Hungate tubes) under a N2-CO2 gas mixture (4:1, v/v), seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
To complete medium, aseptically add the following solutions to the medium (per 10 ml):
Solution B (see below) 0.1 ml
1.0 M Sodium acetate (filter-sterilized) 0.2 ml
5% NaHCO3 solution (filter-sterilized) 0.4 ml
Solution B:
CaCl2 0.1 g
MgCl2·6H2O 0.5 g
Distilled water 10.0 ml
Autoclave under a N2 atmosphere.
