Autoclave Basal medium supplemented with Trace element solution SL-10 and Selenite-tungstate solution under 100% N2 gas. Separately autoclave sucrose and yeast extract, and add to the medium. Filter-sterilize Vitamin solution and NaHCO3 solution, and add to the medium. Distribute the medium into culture vessels under 100% N2 gas and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Before inoculation, reduce the medium with 5% Na2S·9H2O solution (autoclaved under N2 gas). Readjust pH to 7.7 - 7.9, if necessary.
