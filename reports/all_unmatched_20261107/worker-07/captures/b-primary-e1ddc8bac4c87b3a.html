1431   SEMI-SOLID AUTOTROPHIC GROWTH MEDIUM FOR GAMMAPROTEOBACTERIA

NaCl  37.8     g
MgCl2·6H2O  5.4     g
Na2SO4  5.4     g
KCl  0.9     g
CaCl2·2H2O  0.5     g
NH4Cl  0.3     g
NaHCO3  1.26    g
Modified Wolfe’s mineral solution (see Medium No. 915)  5.0     ml
Resazurin  0.2     mg
Distilled water  1.0     L
Agarose  1.6     g
Mix components thoroughly, except agarose, and adjust pH to 7.0. Add agarose to the medium and autoclave under a N2 gas atmosphere. After cooling to 50℃, add the following solutions to the medium aseptically and anaerobically (autoclaved or *filter-sterilized). Keep the medium temperature at 50℃ until the medium is distributed into sterile screw-capped test tubes as described below:
0.5 M Potassium phosphate buffer (pH 7.0)  1.8     ml
Vitamin solution* (see Medium No. 915)  0.5     ml
0.01 M FeSO4·7H2O solution (in 0.02 N HCl)  3.0     ml
40% Na2S2O3·5H2O solution  3.0     ml
5% L-Cysteine·HCl·H2O solution  8.0     ml
Adjust pH to 7.0 with sterile HCl or NaOH solution. Dispense the medium to sterile screw-capped test tubes (typically 11 ml medium into sterile Hungate tubes) under air and cap. Allow to stand until the medium is solidified and a O2-gradient formed (as evidenced by a pink upper oxic zone and a colorless lower anoxic zone). Inoculate cells just below the pink-colorless interface in the medium tube using a syringe.
Comment: The medium tubes can be stocked under a N2 gas atmosphere. In this case, prior to use, fill the head space with air and allow to stand until the O2 gradient is formed.
