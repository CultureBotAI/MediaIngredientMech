1107 LIQUID BASAL MEDIUM FOR METHANOSARCINA
KH2PO4 0.5 g
NH4Cl 0.4 g
MgCl2·6H2O 0.4 g
CaCl2·2H2O 0.05 g
NaCl 0.4 g
MOPS 4.2 g
Trace element solution (see below) 1.0 ml
Resazurin 1.0 mg
Distilled water 930.0 ml
Mix components thoroughly, adjust pH to 7.2 and autoclave under a N2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) gas atmosphere. After cooling, aseptically and anaerobically add the following solutions (autoclaved or *filter-sterilized):
8% NaHCO3 solution* 50.0 ml
Vitamin solution* (see Medium No. 944) 10.0 ml
50% (v/v) Methanol solution 10.0 ml
Aseptically distribute the medium into culture vessels under the same gas mixture and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Finally, add per liter the following solutions from anaerobic stocks (autoclaved and stored under a N2 atmosphere):
5% L-Cysteine·HCl·H2O solution 10.0 ml
5% Na2S·9H2O solution 10.0 ml
Readjust pH to 7.2, if necessary.
