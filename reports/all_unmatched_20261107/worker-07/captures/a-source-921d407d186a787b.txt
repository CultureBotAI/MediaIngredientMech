Mix ingredients except for Na2CO3 and cysteine-HCl, dispense the medium into suitable culture vessels under a stream of N2/CO2 (80/20), seal with butyl rubber stoppers, and autoclave at 115℃ for 25 min. Aseptically and anaerobically add filter-sterile Na2CO3 and cysteine-HCl solutions, and adjust pH to 7.0 with 3 N HCl.
