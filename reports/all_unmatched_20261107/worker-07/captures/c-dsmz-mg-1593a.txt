1593a: Cyanobacteria Medium BG11+ (TES buffered)

Final pH: 8.0
Final volume: 1000 ml


   Mg(SO4) x 7 H2O (7.5 g/l stock solution)                  10.00   ml
   CaCl2 x 2 H2O (3.6 g/l stock solution)                    10.00   ml
