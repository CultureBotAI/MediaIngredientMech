Directions

Dissolve 35.4g in 1 litre of distilled water and bring to the boil to dissolve completely. Distribute
into final containers and sterilize by autoclaving at 121°C for 15 minutes.

Physical Characteristics

Straw, free-flowing powder
Colour on reconstitution – straw/green
Moisture level - less than or equal to 7%
pH - 6.8 ± 0.2 at 25°C
Clarity - clear
Deposit - black particles (Haemin)

Microbiological Tests Using Optimum Inoculum Dilution

Control Medium: Tryptone Soya Agar or Columbia Blood Agar Base enriched with 5% v/v horse blood,
where appropriate.
