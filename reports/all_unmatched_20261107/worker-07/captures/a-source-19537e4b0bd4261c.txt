552 SULFATE REDUCING BACTERIUM MEDIUM WITH LACTATE
KH2PO4 0.5 g
NH4Cl 1.0 g
Na2SO4 1.0 g
CaCl2·2H2O 0.1 g
MgSO4·7H2O 2.0 g
NaCl 10.0 g
Yeast extract (BD-Difco) 0.5 g
Vitamin solution (see below) 1.0 ml
FeCl2 solution (see Medium No. 187) 1.0 ml
Trace element solution (see Medium No. 187) 1.0 ml
L-Sodium lactate 2.3 g
Resazurin 1.0 mg
L-Cysteine·H2O·HCl 0.5 g
Distilled water 1.0 L
Mix ingredients except L-sodium lactate and L-cysteine·H2O·HCl, adjust pH to 7.5 and autoclave under a N2 atmosphere. Separately autoclave L-sodium lactate and L-cysteine·H2O·HCl in 10 ml distilled water under a N2 atmosphere and add to the medium. Readjust pH to 7.5 with 1 N NaOH (from a sterile stock solution).
For preparation of solid medium, add 15 g/L agar (BD-Difco).
Vitamin solution:
Biotin 10.0 µg
p-Aminobenzoic acid 40.0 µg
Nicotinic acid 100.0 µg
Distilled water 1.0 L
