687. COLUMBIA BLOOD AGAR


Columbia Blood Agar Base
 Bacto Panton (Peptone)                                            10.0    g
 Bacto Bitone (Peptone)                                            10.0    g
 Tryptic Digest of beef heart                                       3.0    g
 Corn starch                                                        1.0    g
 NaCl                                                               5.0    g
 Agar                                                              15.0    g

Defribinated Blood

1. Suspend 44 g Columbia Blood Agar Base in 1l deionized water. Heat to boiling to
dissolve completely.

2. Sterilize in autoclave for 15 min at 121C.

3. Cool to 45C. Add 5% sterile defibrinated blood and mix well.
