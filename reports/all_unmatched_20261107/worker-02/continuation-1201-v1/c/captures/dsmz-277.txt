277. CHERRY AGAR (CBS formula)


Add 1 l water to 200.0 g pulp of sour stone cherries.
Heat to boiling and simmer gently for 2 h. Strain through cloth and sterilize at 110C for
30 minutes. Dissolve 20.0 g agar in 800 ml water and sterilize. Add 200 ml cherry
extract, distribute in presterilized tubes and sterilize for 5 min at 120C.
Final pH 3.8 - 4.6.
