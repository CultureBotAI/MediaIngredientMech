1. Dissolve ingredients except carbonate, trimethylamine, DTT, cysteine, and vitamins.
Sparge the medium with an 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture for at least 30-45 minutes
to make it anoxic. Then, dispense it under the same gas atmosphere into anoxic
Hungate-type tubes or serum vials and autoclave. Add trimethylamine, DTT, cysteine, and
vitamins from sterile anoxic stock solutions prepared under 100% N2 gas and carbonate
from a sterile anoxic stock solution prepared under 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture.
Solutions of DTT and vitamins should be sterilized by filtration. Adjust the pH of the
complete medium to 7.4 with an anoxic, sterile solution of 5% w/v Na2CO3, if necessary.
2. Note: A white precipitate that forms after autoclaving of the mineral base will dissolve
again after storage at room temperature for 2 - 3 days.
