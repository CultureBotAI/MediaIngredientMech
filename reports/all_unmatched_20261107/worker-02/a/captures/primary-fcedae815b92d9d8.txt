Sterilize by filtration under a N 2 atmosphere. Prepare Na 2 S·9H 2 O as a 5% solution, and autoclave under a N 2 atmosphere. Aseptically and anaerobically add sterile sulfur to the medium. Prior to inoculation, add the sterile Na 2 S·9H 2 O solution, and readjust pH to 6.5, if necessary.                   *Salt base solution       NaCl 13.85 g       MgSO 4 ·7H 2 O 3.5 g       MgCl 2 ·6H 2 O 2.75 g       KCl 0.325 g       NaBr 0.05 g       H 3 BO 3 15 mg       SrCl 2 ·6H 2 O 7.5 mg       KI 0.05 mg       CaCl 2 0.75 g       KH 2 PO 4 0.5 g       (NH 4 ) 2 Ni(SO 4 ) 2 ·6H 2 O 2 mg       Distilled water 1 L                   **Trace minerals       Nitrilotriacetic acid 1.5 g       MgSO 4 ·7H 2 O 3 g       MnSO 4 ·xH 2 O 0.5 g       NaCl 1 g       FeSO 4 ·7H 2 O 0.1 g       CoSO 4 ·7H 2 O 0.1 g       CaCl 2 ·2H 2 O 0.1 g       ZnSO 4 ·7H 2 O 0.1 g       CuSO 4 ·5H 2 O 0.01 g       AlK(SO 4 ) 2 0.01 g       H 3 BO 3 0.01 g       Na 2 MoO 4 ·2H 2 O 0.01 g       Distilled water 1 L     Dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH solution.
