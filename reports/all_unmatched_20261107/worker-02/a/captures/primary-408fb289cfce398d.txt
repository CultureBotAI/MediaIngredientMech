Medium description: for NLS 7
The growth medium contains the following components (per liter):

Solution A                                                       50.0       ml
Solution B                                                       50.0       ml
Solution C (Acidic Trace Elements Solution)                      0.20       ml
Solution D (Alkaline Trace Elements Solution)                    0.50       ml
Solution E (Wolfe's Vitamin Solution)                           10.00       ml
Bring up to 1 liter

Media preparation instructions:



Combine the distilled water and Solutions A-D in an Erlenmeyer flask and dispense into
glass serum bottles.
Seal the serum bottles with butyl rubber stoppers and crimp seals. Using a syringe and
needle, inject pure 10% methane.
Autoclave the serum bottles for 20 minutes at 121 °C.
Upon cooling to room temperature, add solution E from a filter-sterilized stock solution.
The final pH of the medium should be 7.0
