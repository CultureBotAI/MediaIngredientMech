Medium description:
The growth medium contains the following components (per liter):

Solution 1 (20X Basal Salts Solution)                           50.0      mL
Solution 2 (Wolfe's Vitamin Solution)                           10.0      mL
Solution 3 (Acidic Trace Elements Solution)                      0.5      mL
Solution 4 (Alkaline Trace Elements Solution)                    0.2      mL
Solution 5 (40X Buffer Solution) =                              25.0      mL
Distilled water                                               914.30      ml

Media preparation instructions:



Combine the distilled water and Solutions 1,3,4 in an Erlenmeyer flask and dispense into
glass serum bottles.
Flush the headspace of the serum bottles with pure N2. Seal the serum bottles with
butyl rubber stoppers and crimp seals. Using a syringe and needle, inject pure methane
and oxygen to final headspace concentrations of 5-10% CH4 and 21% O2.
Autoclave the serum bottles for 20 minutes at 121 °C.
Upon cooling to room temperature, add Solutions 2 and 5 from a filter-sterilized stock
solution.
The final pH of the medium should be 7.8 – 7.9.
