Dissolve ingredients except NaHCO3, fructose, cysteine and sodium sulfide, bring to a boil for a few minutes and cool to room temperature under a N2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) gas mixture. Add NaHCO3 (solid) and equilibrate the medium with the gas until pH reaches ca. 7.4. Then distribute and autoclave under the same gas atmosphere. Before use, adjust pH to 8.2 by adding sterile anaerobic Na2CO3 solution (ca. 0.25 ml of 5% Na2CO3 per 10 ml medium) and add fructose, cysteine and sodium sulfide from anaerobic sterile stock solutions.
