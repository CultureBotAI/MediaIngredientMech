Prepare the medium, and adjust the pH to 7.6 with 5M NaOH.
Autoclave and when the medium has cooled add 2.5 ml of the phosphate solution, 0.5 ml
of the calcium chloride solution, and 0.25 ml of the Fe/Mn solution. This strain will not
grow in normal media for halobacteria, such as DSM medium 372!
