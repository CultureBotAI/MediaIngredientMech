Single stock phase: Prepare the single stocks as reported above. Single stocks are prepared either in purified H
2
O or in the Non-Nutrient Medium (NNM) component of RS Medium (see accompanying protocol on MediaDive). Filtration is performed with a 0.22 µm pore size.
3
Single stock phase: Notes for Methyl cellulose: prepare the single stock in H
2
O. It is known that Methyl cellulose is soluble in cold water and insoluble in hot water. In order to prepare this stock, add the relative amount and shake the single stock from time to time for 20 minutes. Leave the stock in cold water overnight. Its color should be white. The day after, after autoclaving, it will result like a white solid, not liquid. Leave it at RT until it becomes liquid and transparent. Store at 4C.
4
Single stock phase: Notes for Pectin: prepare the single stock in H
2
O. To dissolve, use a hot plate heater with a magnetic stirrer for 20 minutes before autoclaving. Store at 4C.
5

8
Mix solutions phase: Mix solution 4 (Amino Acids): add 2.25 ml from the MEM 50X Amino Acids solution from Sigma and 2.27 ml from the MEM 100X Amino Acids solution from Sigma. Add 2.27 ml of the single stock of Glutamine. Fill to 50 ml with NNM. Filter via syringe through a 0.22 µm pore size. Store at 4C.

NOTES: The MEM 50X AA solution contains Arginine (6.32 g/L), Cysteine HCl (1.56 g/L), Histidine (2.10 g/L), L-Isoleucine (2.63 g/L), L-Leucine (2.62 g/L), Lysine HCl (3.63 g/L), L-Methionine (0.76 g/L), L-Phenylalanine (1.65 g/L), L-Threonine (2.38 g/L), L-Tryptophan (0.51 g/L), L-Tyrosine (1.80 g/L) and L-Valine (2.34 g/L). 
The MEM 100X AA solution contains L-Alanine (0.89 g/L), Glycine (0.75 g/L), L-Proline (1.15 g/L), L-Asparagine*H
2
O (1.50 g/L), Aspartate (1.33 g/L), Glutamate (1.47 g/L), Serine (1.05 g/L).
9
Mix solutions phase: Mix solution 5 (Carbon Sources): add 45 µl of Glycerol from Sigma (1.26 g/mL). Add 2.27 ml of each carbon source (Sodium pyruvate, D-glucose, N-acetyI-D-glucosamine, D-ribose, Sodium succinate, y-aminobutyric acid, Sodium acetate, Decanoic acid, L-arabinose, L-rhamnose, Fucose, L-mannose, Galactose, Xylan, Sodium alginate, Methyl-cellulose, Pectin, Guanosine and Uracil). Fill to 50 ml with NNM. Filter through a 0.22 µm pore size by using the vacuum filter (not by syringe) because the solution is viscous. Store at 4C.
