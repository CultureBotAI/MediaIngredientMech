Distribute the medium into appropriate culture vessels (e.g., 20 ml of the medium in 120 ml serum bottles) under a H 2 -CO 2 (4:1, v/v) gas mixture, sparge with the same gas mixture for 10 - 15 min, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave. Separately autoclave concentrated (100- or 200-fold) L-cysteine·HCl·H 2 O and FeCl 2 ·4H 2 O solutions under a N 2 gas atmosphere.  Prior to inoculation, add the sterile L-cysteine·HCl·H 2 O and FeCl 2 ·4H 2 O solutions.  Pressurize the inoculated vessels to 100 kPa H 2 -CO 2 (4:1, v/v) and cultivate without shaking. Poorly crystalline iron(III) oxide: Dissolve 108 g FeCl 3 in 1.0 L distilled water.  Adjust pH to 7.0 with 10 N NaOH.  Allow the suspension to sit for 30 min, then readjust pH to 7.0. Centrifuge the suspension at 5,000 x g for 20 min and pour off supernatant. Wash the precipitate with distilled water by resuspending in distilled water followed by the centrifugation.  Repeat the washing at least 5 times, then, resuspend in 1.0 L distilled water.  Use the Fe(III) suspension as 0.67 M solution.  More precisely, check the concentration of Cl - to be less than 1 mM and determine the concentration of Fe(III) by using hydroxylamine as described by Lovley and Phillips (Appl. Environ. Microbiol. 54, 751-757, 1986). Trace mineral solution: Use Trace minerals of Medium No. 151 supplemented with 0.025 g (final) Na 2 MoO 4 ·2H 2 O, 0.024 g NiCl 2 ·6H 2 O and 0.025 g Na 2 WO 4 ·2H 2 O (per liter).
