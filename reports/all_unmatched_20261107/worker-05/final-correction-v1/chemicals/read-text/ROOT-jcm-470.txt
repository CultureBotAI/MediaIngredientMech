Unless otherwise stated, sterilize media by autoclaving at 121°C for 15 min. 470   VULCANIBACILLUS MEDIUM Sea salts (Sigma) 30.0 g NaNO 3 1.0 g Wolfe's mineral elixir (see below) 2.0 ml VOSO 4 · x H 2 O 0.05 mg Sodium pyruvate 1.0 g Resazurin 0.5 mg FeSO 4 ·7H 2 O 0.1 g NaHCO 3 1.0 g Yeast extract 0.5 g Trace vitamins (see Medium No. 197 ) 20.0 ml Distilled water 1.0 L Dissolve ingredients except FeSO 4 ·7H 2 O, NaHCO 3 , yeast extract and vitamins, and adjust pH to 7.0.  Bring to a boil for 3 min, then cool to room temperature under a N 2 gas stream.  Dispense the medium under the same gas atmosphere into culture vessels, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.  Add sterile anaerobic stock solutions of FeSO 4 ·7H 2 O (1%, in 0.1 N H 2 SO 4 ), NaHCO 3 (8%, under a N 2 ·CO 2 gas mixture), yeast extract (10%) and vitamins (filter-sterilized) to the medium.  Adjust final pH of the medium to 7.0 - 7.2.  Add 10 - 20 mg sodium dithionite per liter [e.g., from 5% (w/v) solution, freshly prepared under N 2 and filter-sterilized], if the medium is not completely reduced after inoculation.
