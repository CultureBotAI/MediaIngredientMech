Separately autoclave Na 2 CO 3 under a N 2 /CO 2 (80/20) atmosphere. Separately autoclave sodium lactate and Na 2 S·9H 2 O under a N 2 atmosphere. Sterilize vitamin solution by filtration. To complete the medium, add Na 2 CO 3 ,  sodium lactate, vitamin solution and Na 2 S·9H 2 O. Adjust pH of the complete medium to around 7.5, if necessary.
