Unless otherwise stated, sterilize media by autoclaving at 121°C for 15 min. 355   METHANOSPHAERA MEDIUM Rumen fluid, clarified (see below) 100.0 ml Yeast extract (BD-Difco) 2.0 g Trypticase peptone (BD-BBL) 2.0 g Sodium acetate 0.5 g Sodium formate 0.5 g Trace minerals (see Medium No. 151 ) 10.0 ml Trace vitamins (see Medium No. 197 ) 1.0 ml Na 2 SeO 4 0.19 mg NiCl 2 ·6H 2 O 0.7 mg FeSO 4 ·7H 2 O 3.0 mg KH 2 PO 4 2.8 g K 2 HPO 4 0.6 g (NH 4 ) 2 SO 4 0.3 g NH 4 Cl 1.0 g NaCl 0.6 g MgSO 4 ·7H 2 O 0.15 g CaCl 2 ·2H 2 O 0.076 g Methanol 5.0 ml Resazurin 1.0 mg L-Cysteine·HCl·H 2 O 0.875 g Na 2 S·9H 2 O 0.375 g Distilled water 900.0 ml Mix ingredients, except methanol, L-cysteine·HCl·H 2 O and  Na 2 S·9H 2 O, and adjust pH to 7.0.  Bring to a boil for 5-10 sec,  then, cool down under a stream of N 2 and add methanol to the medium.    Dispense the medium into suitable culture vessels (e.g., 20 ml of the medium  in a 100-ml serum bottle) under a stream of N 2 and seal.  After autoclaving, stand the medium overnight.  Separately autoclave L-cysteine·HCl·H 2 O and Na 2 S·9H 2 O as 5% solutions, respectively, under a N 2 atmosphere.  Prior to inoculation, replace the gas phase with H 2 -CO 2 (80:20, v/v), and add the  L-cysteine·HCl·H 2 O and Na 2 S·9H 2 O solutions aseptically and anaerobically.  Pressurize the inoculated bottles to 200 kPa H 2 -CO 2 (80:20, v/v). To prepare the clarified rumen fluid, preheat rumen content at 120C for 15 min.
