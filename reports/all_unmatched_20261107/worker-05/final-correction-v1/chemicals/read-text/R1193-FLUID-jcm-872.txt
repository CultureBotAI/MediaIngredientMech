Cool down under a stream of H 2 -CO 2 (80:20, v/v) and add NaHCO 3 to the medium.   Dispense the medium into culture vessels under a stream of H 2 -CO 2 (80:20, v/v), seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.  Prior to inoculation, add per liter the following solutions (autoclaved and stored under a N 2 atmosphere): 5% Na 2 S·9H 2 O solution 10.0 ml 5% L-Cysteine·HCl·H 2 O solution 10.0 ml Pressurize inoculated vessels to 200 kPa with a H 2 -CO 2 (80:20, v/v) gas mixture. Sludge fluid: Add 0.4% yeast extract to sludge from an anaerobic digester and gassing with a N 2 gas for a few minutes.  Incubate it at 37C for 24 hr under a N 2 atmosphere.  Then centrifuge the sludge at 13,000 x g and autoclave the resulting clear supernatant in screw-capped vessels under a N 2 atmosphere. The sludge fluid can be stored at room temperature in the dark.
