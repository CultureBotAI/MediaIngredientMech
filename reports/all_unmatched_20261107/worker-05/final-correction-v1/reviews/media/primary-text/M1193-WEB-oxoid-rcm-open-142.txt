L18@P0: Summary and Explanation
L19@P0: Anaerobic bacteria are important pathogens that can cause a variety of infections in humans. The site of anaerobic infection is
L20@P0: commonly the site of normal colonization. Anaerobic means "life without air." Anaerobic bacteria grow in places which completely,
L21@P0: or almost completely, lack oxygen. The spectrum of infections ranges from superficial abscesses to life-threatening infections.
L22@P0: Anaerobic microorganisms, including pathogens such as Clostridium species, Bacteroides fragilis, Fusobacterium necrophorum
L23@P0: and Fusobacterium nucleatum, can be highly infectious and can cause life-threatening diseases. Anaerobic bacteria cause
L24@P0: infections which can be severe and life-threatening1,2 (Public Health England, 2015a; 2015b). Anaerobes are endogenous in
L25@P0: nature, as they are a primary component of the flora of mucus membranes and gastrointestinal tract. However, they are often
L26@P0: difficult to isolate and recognise due to their fastidious nature and overgrowth of facultative anaerobes such as coliforms. This
L27@P0: can lead to delays in diagnosis, and therefore delays in determining appropriate therapy, which may lead to clinical failures3
L28@P0: (Brook, 2016).
L29@P0: Principle of Method
L30@P0: Reinforced Clostridial Medium (CM0149B, CM0149T): Peptone supplies nutrients and glucose is an easily fermentable energy
L31@P0: source. Yeast extract supplies B vitamins and other factors essential for promoting growth. Soluble starch acts to neutralize toxic
L32@P0: metabolic by-products. Sodium chloride maintains the osmotic equilibrium. Cysteine hydrochloride is the reducing agent and
L33@P0: sodium acetate a buffering agent. The presence of agar in a small quantity reduces the oxygen permeability of the medium.
L34@P0: Reinforced Clostridial Agar (CM0151B): Peptone provides nutrients and glucose is an easily fermentable energy source. Yeast
L35@P0: extract supplies B vitamins and other factors essential for promoting growth. Soluble starch acts to neutralize toxic metabolic by
L36@P0: products. Sodium chloride maintains the osmotic equilibrium. Cysteine hydrochloride is the reducing agent and sodium acetate
L37@P0: a buffering agent. Agar is a solidifying agent.

L156@P2: Performance Characteristics
L157@P2: Accuracy has been demonstrated through review of the QC data. Correct detection of Clostridium species and other anaerobes
L158@P2: from clinical samples (e.g., faeces, blood, and wounds) is confirmed by the inclusion of a well-characterised isolates in the QC
L159@P2: processes performed as part of the manufacture of each batch of the devices. The precision of Reinforced Clostridial Medium
L160@P2: (CM0149B, CM0149T) devices was demonstrated by an overall pass rate of 100% obtained for the product over one and a half
L161@P2: years of testing (11.JAN.2022 – 15.AUG.2022; 10 batches). The precision of Reinforced Clostridial Agar (CM0151B) devices was
L162@P2: demonstrated by an overall pass rate of 80% obtained for the product over one year of testing (08.07.2021 – 15.08.2022; 10
L163@P2: batches). An overall pass-rate of 80% (based on data from the most recent 10 batches) provides sufficient data on accuracy
L164@P2: considering the low risk of the device and it is not reflective of the product that is released for clinical use. With the normal variation
L165@P2: in the manufacturing process, a small number of failures may occur, however, there is no risk to the end user as batches will be
L166@P2: captured in QC if out of specification. This shows that the performance is reproducible.

L237@P4: Souhrn a vysvětlení
L238@P4: Anaerobní bakterie jsou významnými patogeny, které mohou u lidí způsobovat různé infekce. Místo anaerobní infekce je běžně
L239@P4: místem normální kolonizace. Anaerobní znamená „život bez vzduchu“. Anaerobní bakterie rostou v místech, kde zcela nebo
L240@P4: téměř zcela chybí kyslík. Spektrum infekcí sahá od povrchových abscesů až po život ohrožující infekce. Anaerobní
L241@P4: mikroorganismy, včetně patogenů, jako jsou druhyClostridium, Bacteroides fragilis, Fusobacterium necrophorum
L242@P4: a Fusobacterium nucleatum, mohou být vysoce infekční a mohou způsobovat život ohrožující onemocnění. Anaerobní bakterie
L243@P4: způsobují infekce, které mohou být závažné a život ohrožující1,2 (Public Health England, 2015a; 2015b). Anaeroby jsou endogenní
L244@P4: povahy, protože jsou primární složkou flóry sliznic a gastrointestinálního traktu. Jejich izolace a rozeznání jsou však často obtížné
L245@P4: kvůli jejich náročné povaze a přemnožení fakultativních anaerobů, jako jsou koliformní bakterie. To může vést ke zpoždění
L246@P4: diagnózy, a tedy ke zpoždění při určování vhodné terapie a následnému klinickému selhání3 (Brook, 2016).
