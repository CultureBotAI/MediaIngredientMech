L23@P0: Principle and Interpretation:
L24@P0: Brewer (1) formulated Fluid Thioglycollate Medium for rapid cultivation of aerobes as well as anaerobes
L25@P0: including microaerophiles by adding a reducing agent and small amount of agar. The BP (2), EP (3),
L26@P0: USP (4), and AOAC (5) have recommended the media for sterility testing of antibiotics, biologicals and
L27@P0: foods and for determining the phenol coefficient and sporicidal effect of disinfectants. However, it is
L28@P0: intended for the examination of clear liquid or water-soluble materials. Fluid Thioglycollate Medium is
L29@P0: also routinely used to check the sterility of stored blood in blood banks (6).
L30@P0: Dextrose, tryptone, yeast extract, L-cystine provide the growth factors necessary for bacterial growth.
L31@P0: Sodium thioglycollate and L-cystine are reducing agent that prevent the accumulation of peroxides
L32@P0: which are lethal to some microorganisms. They allows Clostridium to grow in this medium even under
L33@P0: aerobic conditions (7). Resazurin is an oxidation-reduction indicator, being pink when oxidized and
L34@P0: colorless when reduced. Also the small amount of agar used in the medium favors the growth of
L35@P0: aerobes as well as anaerobes in the medium, independend of absence or presence of sodium
L36@P0: thioglycollate (1). Sodium thioglycollate act as a reducing agent and neutralizes the toxic effects of
L37@P0: mercurial preservatives and peroxides formed in the medium, thereby promoting anaerobiosis, and
L38@P0: making the medium suitable to test materials containing heavy metals. (8,9). Any increase in the
L39@P0: oxygen content is indicated by a color change of redox indicator, resazurin to red (6,10,11). The small
