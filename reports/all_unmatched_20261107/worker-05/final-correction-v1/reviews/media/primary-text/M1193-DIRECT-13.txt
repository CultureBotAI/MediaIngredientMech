                  CZAPEK DOX LIQUID MEDIUM (MODIFIED) CM0095
CZAPEK DOX LIQUID MEDIUM (MODIFIED)                                                    CM0095

Typical Formula*

Sodium nitrate                                 grams per litre                          2.0
Potassium chloride                                                                      0.5
Magnesium glycerophosphate                                                              0.5
Ferrous sulphate                                                                        0.01
Potassium sulphate                                                                      0.35
Sucrose                                                                                30.0

* adjusted as required to meet performance standards

Directions

Dissolve 33.4g in 1 litre of distilled water. Mix well and distribute into final containers. Sterilize by
autoclaving at 121°C for 15 minutes.

Physical Characteristics

White, free-flowing powder
Colour on reconstitution - colourless
Moisture level - less than or equal to 7%
pH 6.8 ± 0.2 at 25°C
Clarity - clear

Microbiological Tests Using Optimum Inoculum Dilution

Control Media: Sabouraud Dextrose Agar or Tryptone Soya Agar, where appropriate

Reactions after incubation at 25°C for 5 days

Medium is challenged with greater than 1E+03 colony-forming units

Aspergillus brasiliensis       ATCC®9642               White, surface mycelia, black spores
Penicillium rubens             ATCC®9179               White, surface mycelia, green spores

Medium is challenged with greater than 1E+05 colony-forming units

Candida albicans               ATCC®10231              Suspended growth

Medium is challenged with greater than 1E+04 colony-forming units

Bacillus cereus                ATCC®10876              No growth or growth at base of tube

Degree of growth and spore production shall be comparable to the standard.
