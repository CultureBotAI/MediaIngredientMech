This nutritional supplement provides vitamins, nucleotides, minerals, and peptides in a variety of sizes to any culture formulation. Customers have used Bacto Neopeptone as a component of culture media in the cultivation of human pathogens, notably Bordetella pertussis and group A streptococci, as well as Borrelia burgdorferi and Clostridium species, to produce vaccines for animal health applications.
Features:
• Enzymatic digest of bovine (milk) and porcine protein
• Animal origin nutritional supplement
• Contains a wide variety of peptide sizes in combination with vitamins, nucleotides, and minerals
Applications of Bacto Neopeptone include:
• Recommended for use in media for detection of fungi
• Reported to provide nutrients for support of spirochetes and protozoa
• Microbial fermentation
• Production of vaccines for animal health applications
