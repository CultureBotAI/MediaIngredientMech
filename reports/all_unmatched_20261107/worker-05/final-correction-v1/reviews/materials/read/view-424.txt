                                Composition
 KP buffer*10ml
 MgCl2·6H2O0.75g
 CaCl2·2H2O0.15g
 NH4Cl0.54g
 Na2S2O33.2g
 Bacto Yeast Extract (Difco)0.1g
 Peptone (BBL)1g
 Glucose0.2g
 Sodium succinate0.2g
 Trace elements solution**2ml
 Vitamin solution***2ml
 Na2CO30.5g
 Distilled water1L
  Mix ingredients except KP buffer and vitamin solution. Dispense the medium into suitable culture vessels under a stream of H2/CO2/O2 (75/20/5) and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Separately autoclave KP buffer under a N2 atmosphere. Sterilize vitamin solution by filtration. Prior to inoculation, aseptically add KP buffer and vitamin solution. Pressurize the inoculated vessels to 150 kPa with H2/CO2/O2.
