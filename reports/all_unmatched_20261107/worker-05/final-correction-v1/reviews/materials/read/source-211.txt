Mix components and adjust pH to 6.0.  Bubble the medium with N2 for 5 min, then distribute the medium into culture vessels under N2, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.  After cooling, add the following solutions aseptically and anaerobically (autoclaved and stored under a N2 gas atmosphere):
