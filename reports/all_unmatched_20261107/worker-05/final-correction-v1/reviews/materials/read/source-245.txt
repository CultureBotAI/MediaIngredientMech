1054   MAIZE MEAL-SARDINE AGAR


 Maize meal  20.0      g  

 Small dried sardine  20.0      g  

 Agar  20.0      g  

 Distilled water   1.0      L   

Boil maize meal and dried sardine with 500 ml of distilled water for 20 min. Filtered through cloth, make up to 1 L, add agar, and boil till dissolved.
