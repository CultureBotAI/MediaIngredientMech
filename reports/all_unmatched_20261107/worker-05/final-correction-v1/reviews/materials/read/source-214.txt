Mix ingredients except methanol, NaHCO3 and Na2S·9H2O, bring to a boil and cool down under a stream of N2 gas.  Add methanol to the medium and distribute into culture vessels (e.g., 20 ml of the medium in 120 ml serum bottles) under a stream of H2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas mixture, seal with butyl rubber stoppers, and autoclave.  Separately autoclave 5% Na2S·9H2O solution under a N2 atmosphere.  Filer-sterilize 8% NaHCO3 solution and store under a N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas mixture.  Before inoculation, add the sterile NaHCO3 and Na2S·9H2O solutions to the medium, and readjust pH to 7.0, if necessary. Pressurize the inoculated bottles to 200 kPa H2-CO2 (4:1, v/v).
