963   DESULFOTOMACULUM TONGENSE MEDIUM

Mix components thoroughly, bring to a boil and cool down to room temperature under a N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas stream.  Dispense the medium into culture vessels under a N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas mixture, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.  After cooling, add per liter the following solutions from anaerobic stocks (autoclaved or *filter-sterilized):  
 Trace vitamins* (see Medium No. 197)                  10.0     ml  
 8% NaHCO3 solution*                                 20.0     ml  
 5% Na2S·9H2O solution                      10.0     ml  
Check final pH to be 7.0 - 7.2.
