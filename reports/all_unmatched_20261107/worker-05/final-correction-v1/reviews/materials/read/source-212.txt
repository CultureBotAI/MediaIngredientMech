 FeSO4·7H2O                                   0.1     g  

 NaHCO3                                                1.0     g  

 Yeast extract                                            0.5     g  

 Trace vitamins (see Medium No. 197)                   20.0     ml  

 Distilled water                                          1.0     L  

Dissolve ingredients except FeSO4·7H2O, NaHCO3, yeast extract and vitamins, and adjust pH to 7.0.  Bring to a boil for 3 min, then cool to room temperature under a N2 gas stream.  Dispense the medium under the same gas atmosphere into culture vessels, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.  Add sterile anaerobic stock solutions of FeSO4·7H2O (1%, in 0.1 N H2SO4), NaHCO3 (8%, under a N2·CO2 gas mixture), yeast extract (10%) and vitamins (filter-sterilized) to the medium.  Adjust final pH of the medium to 7.0 - 7.2.  Add 10 - 20 mg sodium dithionite per liter [e.g., from 5% (w/v) solution, freshly prepared under N2 and filter-sterilized], if the medium is not completely reduced after inoculation.
