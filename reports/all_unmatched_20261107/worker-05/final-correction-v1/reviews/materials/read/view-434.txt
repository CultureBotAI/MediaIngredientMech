       Soilwater: Peat Medium
Lacks supplements; suitable for most acid loving algae.
Directions
For 200 mL Total
1. Combine components.
2. Cover the medium container and steam for 2 consecutive days, 3 hours on each
   day. Pasteurization is a gradual rising of temperature to approximately 95 °C in 15
   minutes. Then increased just over 98 °C for the 3 hour duration. Cooling occurs
   gradually at room temperature.
3. Refrigerate 24 hours or more and bring to room temperature before use.
