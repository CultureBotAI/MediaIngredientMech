This assessment concerns the identity of the inherited peptone part, not approval of a complete culturing recipe. An applicable original source explicitly naming unqualified peptone can support generic peptone without supplier details, concentrations, complete formulation, or a catalogue number. None of those missing details alone justifies an identity hold.

The ten whole-claim holds in the ledger concern absent or conflicting **applicable named-part evidence**. “Recover recipe/formulation” identifies one way to obtain that evidence; it does not impose complete-recipe recovery as a new requirement. A source-specific statement identifying the component would also suffice, with independent review of its scope.

For PY-cellobiose, Moore 1976 supplies occurrence-specific generic support for Eubacterium siraeum. That support is retained. The same owner also absorbs a Clostridium polysaccharolyticum observation whose applicable named-part evidence was not recovered. The review therefore does not transfer that one occurrence's support to the whole owner.

For PY-maltose, Moore 1976 explicitly establishes C. leptum's use of the named medium, but the inspected passage does not identify its digest part. The current DSMZ 104c/DSM753 recipe explicitly contains two distinct digest parts. This is a substantive representation mismatch with the inherited single peptone entry, not merely a concern that a webpage is newer.

For PY-pectin, current ATCC 1223 identifies Polypeptone BD211910 in a complete named formulation. Its identity with the historical source's PY-pectin preparation was not established. A shared organism does not by itself establish the same preparation. The limitation is this missing preparation link; the specific product evidence remains valid within ATCC 1223.

For Fastidious, the original paper attributes the FAB preparation to Oxoid while the inherited assertion relies on LabM/Neogen. Separate Anaerobe Systems PYG tests in the original paper are not FAB composition evidence. PEPTONE-SOURCE-001 records that precise applicability discrepancy; the review does not claim peptone absence.

All remaining claims lack inspected original text establishing the interpreted peptone part in the actual source context. The proposed UNMAPPED action preserves the existing interpreted literal and historical evidence without falsely changing an abbreviation into a directly observed ingredient. It is a bounded future curation recommendation, not an applied fix or a new owner, role, or supported-export approval.
