# Worker 05 material-source review

The material fragment accounts for all 61 assigned cases and preserves every frozen occurrence, raw source identifier, current owner hash and embedded hold. Decisions distinguish commercial products, extraction inputs, biological preparations, complete media, stocks and chemical classes. No maintained input or publication surface was changed.

Four isolated proposals are in `proposals.json`: reject Neopeptone and Universal peptone aliases on Bacto Peptone; reject the numbered No.3 aliases on unnumbered Proteose Peptone; reject d-salicin on Sucrose; and give the supplier-scoped Sea Salts preparation its own registry identity. The revised sea-salts proposal withdraws both exact and close NCIT claims. Its superseded payload is retained under `superseded/` for review provenance. All proposals preserve source labels, history and release holds. They require coordinator integration and full changed-owner review.

Primary source review also confirms volume-to-mass defects for whole-fat milk and 20% sucrose stock, and preparation flattening for leafy soil, Scharlau proteose-peptone, Oxoid Special peptone and BD Todd-Hewitt's Peptonen. CultureMech issue 408 covers the unit mechanism; issues 150 and 214 cover preparation hierarchy. The latter two commercial formula contexts were recovered from complete TOGO records and are not generic unresolved cases.

The remaining unresolved source identities are Peptone (BBL), unnumbered Bovine albumin fraction, and Peptone (Oxoid). Their decisions state the exact historical product or fraction evidence needed. Bovine fraction V, soybean alternatives, fish preparations, Phytone/Soytone and Hipolypepton remain case-specific material contexts rather than exact generic aliases.

Retrieval failures are preserved as unsuccessful attempts, including invalid MediaDive compound routes, missing JCM pages and inaccessible Bergey chapters. Five historical TOGO records were reused from the original CultureMech archive, retaining its whole-file hash and recorded batch fetch date; exact per-row retrieval times were not recorded. Current supplier product comparisons do not silently establish historical catalog identity.

`integrity-receipt.json` records structural fragment checks. `write_validated_ingredient` accepted all four isolated payloads. Those results establish data shape and hashes, not scientific approval. Independent review is tracked separately, and no roles or complete owner payload are certified by this source review.
