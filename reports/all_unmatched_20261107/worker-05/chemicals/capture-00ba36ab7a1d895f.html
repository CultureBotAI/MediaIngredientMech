COMPLAN ORIGINAL

Discover all the goodness of Complan Original

Tasty served warm or cold, and easy extra nourishment in your favourite recipes.

Complan Original can be easily used to fortify your favourite food or drinks.

With protein which contributes to the growth and maintenance of muscle mass and 26 vitamins and minerals:

  • Vitamin C, zinc and iron which contribute to the normal functioning of the immune system
  • Calcium, vitamin D and magnesium which contribute to the maintenance of normal bones
  • Vitamins B2, B6 and B12 which contribute to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue

Quick and easy to prepare so you can enjoy all of Complan’s carefully developed goodness.
