                                       TAP medium


Stocks                                                                per litre
         (1) NH4Cl                                                    8g
             CaCl2.2H2O                                               1g
             MgSO4.7H2O                                               2g
         (2) K2HPO4                                                   10.8 g
             KH2PO4                                                   5.6 g

         (3) Trace Elements (Hutner’s)
             Na2EDTA.2H2O                                             50 g
             ZnSO4.7H2O                                               22 g
             H3BO3                                                    11.4 g
             MnCl2.4H2O                                               5.1 g
             FeSO4.7H2O                                               5g
             CoCl2.6H2O                                               1.6 g
             CuSO4.5H2O                                               1.16 g
             (NH4)6Mo7O24.4H2O                                        1.1 g

*It is recommended to look up a separate recipe to make Hutner’s trace element solution as
it is a complicated process*

                                                                      per litre
         (4) Tris                                                     242 g
             Glacial acetic acid                                      100 ml


Medium                                                                per litre
         Stock solution 1                                             50 ml
         Stock solution 2                                             10 ml
         Stock solution 3                                             1 ml
         Stock solution 4                                             10 ml


Make up to 1 litre with deionised water. For agar, add 15 g per litre Bacteriological Agar.
Autoclave at 15 psi for 15 minutes.


Reference
Gorman, D., & Levine, R.P. (1965). Cytochrome f and plastocyanin: their sequence in the
photosynthetic electron transport chain of Chlamydomonas reinhardi. Proceedings of the
National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America, 54 6, 1665-9. – adapted for
CCAP
