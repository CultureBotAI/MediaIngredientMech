609: OATMEAL AGAR (A) (ISP-3)

Final pH: 7.2
Final volume: 1000 ml


   Oatmeal (Quaker White Oats)                           20.00        g
   Agar (Difco)                                          18.00        g
   Distilled water                                     1000.00       ml
   Trace salt solution                                    1.00       ml

Adjust pH to 7.2. Cook or steam 20.0 g in 1000.0 ml distilled water for 20 min. Filter
through cheese cloth. Add 18.0 g agar and make up to 1000.0 ml. Add 1 ml of trace salts
solution (see below).




Trace salt solution (from medium 547)
   FeSO4 x 7 H2O                                          0.10        g
   MnCl2 x 4 H2O                                          0.10        g
   ZnSO4 x 7 H2O                                          0.10        g
   Distilled water                                      100.00       ml
