      MY75S (Malt & Yeast Extract - 75% Seawater Agar)
                                         Marine Protozoa



Medium                                                                per litre
         Natural filtered seawater                                    750.0 ml
         Deionised water                                              250.0 ml
         Malt extract                                                 0.1 g
         Yeast extract                                                0.1 g
         Bacteriological agar                                         15.0 g


Make up the 75% seawater and heat (do not bring to boil). Then, add and dissolve the malt
extract and yeast extract. Cool slightly then add the agar and shake to disperse. Autoclave at
15 psi for 15 minutes.


Reference
Catalogue of the UKNCC List of Algae and Protozoa, First Edition, 2001
