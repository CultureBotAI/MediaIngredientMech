148: OATMEAL AGAR

Final volume: 1000 ml


   Oatmeal (Quaker White Oats)                               30.00         g
   Agar                                                      20.00         g
   Distilled water                                         1000.00        ml

Boil 30.0 g of oatmeal with 1.0 L of distilled water over water bath, stirring occasionally, for
1 hr. Filter through cloth, make up to 1.0 L, add agar and boil till dissolved.
