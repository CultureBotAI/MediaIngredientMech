             OXOID QUALITY ASSURANCE PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
                                 NUTRIENT AGAR CM0003

NUTRIENT AGAR                                                                       CM0003

Typical Formula*
‘Lab-Lemco’ powder                            grams per litre                        1.0
Yeast extract                                                                        2.0
Peptone                                                                              5.0
Sodium chloride                                                                      5.0
Agar                                                                                15.0

* adjusted as required to meet performance standards

Directions

Suspend 28g in 1 litre of distilled water. Bring to the boil to dissolve completely. Sterilize by
autoclaving at 121°C for 15 minutes. Cool to 50°C. Mix well and pour into sterile Petri dishes.

Physical Characteristics

Straw, free-flowing powder
Colour on reconstitution - straw 1-2
Moisture level - less than 7%
pH 7.4 ± 0.2 at 25°C
Clarity - clear
Gel strength - firm, comparable to 15.0g/litre of agar

The medium is tested for compatibility using 7% v/v oxalated horse blood, defibrinated horse blood or
defibrinated sheep blood. There shall be no evidence of lysis or darkening, after incubation at 37°C,
25°C and 4°C for 72 hours.

Thermophiles shall be absent after incubation at 55°C for 3 days.

Microbiological Tests Using Optimum Inoculum Dilution

Control Medium: Nutrient Agar

Medium is challenged with 10-100 colony-forming units

Reactions after incubation at 37 ± 2°C for 24 ± 2 hours

Plain plates

Staphylococcus aureus         ATCC® 25923             1-2mm white/straw colonies
Pseudomonas aeruginosa        ATCC® 27853             1-3mm straw colonies

A satisfactory result is represented by recovery of positive strains equal to or greater than 70% of
the control medium.
