Dissolve ingredients except calcium chloride, thiosulfate and sulfide. Sparge medium with
100% N2 gas for at least 30 - 45 min to make it anoxic, then dispense medium under same
gas atmosphere into anoxic Hungate-type tubes or serum vials and autoclave. Add calcium
chloride, thiosulfate and sulfide from sterile anoxic stock solutions prepared under 100% N
2 gas. The pH of the complete medium should be at 6.7 - 6.8.



For DSM 112078: Adjust pH of complete medium to 7.5.
