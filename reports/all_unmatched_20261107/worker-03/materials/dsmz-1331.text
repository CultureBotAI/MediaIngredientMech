1331. COLUMBIA AGAR WITH 5% SHEEP BLOOD


 Columbia Blood Agar Base
 Bacto Panton (Peptone)                                         10.0       g
 Bacto Bitone (Peptone)                                         10.0       g
 Tryptic digest of beef heart                                    3.0       g
 Corn starch                                                     1.0       g
 NaCl                                                            5.0       g
 Agar                                                           15.0       g
 Distilled water                                              1000.0      ml
 Defibrinated Blood                                             50.0      ml

1. Suspend 44 g Columbia Blood Agar Base in 1l deionized water. Heat to boiling to
dissolve completely.

2. Sterilize by autoclaving for 15 min at 121C.

3. Cool to 45C. Add 5% sterile defibrinated sheep blood and mix well.

Rehydrate pellets of freeze-dried ampoules in liquid medium 220 (see below).




220. CASO AGAR (Merck 105458) – without Agar


 Peptone from casein                                            15.0       g
 Peptone from soymeal                                            5.0       g
 NaCl                                                            5.0       g
 Distilled water                                              1000.0      ml

Adjust pH to 7.3. Medium is identical with Tryptone Soya Agar (Oxoid Cm131).
