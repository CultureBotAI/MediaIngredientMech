They are widely used in the laboratory as washing solutions for cells prior to their dissociation or as reaction buffers for biochemical tests (ELISA) and other diagnostic techniques. In microbiology, saline solutions are also used for the preparation of culture media and for the dilution of samples in microbiology tests. Salt solutions are used: for the preparation of organ parts, during the dissociation process, to isolate cell suspensions or to prepare viral transport medium. Because of its negative charge, Bovine Serum Albumin binds water, salts, fatty acids, vitamins and hormones and carries these bound components between tissues and cells. Albumins are frequently used as stabilizers for other solubilized proteins (e.g., labile enzymes). Due to its high stability and less interference with biological activities, BSA is used on many biochemical applications such as Immunochemistry and Western-Blot or ELISA, in which it is used as a blocking reagent.
