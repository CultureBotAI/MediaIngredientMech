             OXOID QUALITY ASSURANCE PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
                                    LIVER BROTH CM0077
LIVER BROTH                                                                        CM0077

Typical Formula*

Infusion from fresh liver                    grams per litre                       23.0
Peptone                                                                            10.0
Potassium phosphate                                                                 1.0
Extracted liver tissue                                                             30.0

* adjusted as required to meet performance standards

Directions

Suspend 64g in 1 litre of distilled water and soak for 15 minutes, with occasional stirring.
Distribute into 18mm diameter tubes to a depth of 50mm, so that the bottom of the tube is filled
with liver particles. Agitate frequently during distribution to keep the liver tissue in suspension.
Sterilize by autoclaving at 115°C for 20 minutes.

Physical Characteristics

Brown granules
Colour on reconstitution - dark brown
Moisture level - less than or equal to 7%
pH - 6.8 ± 0.2 at 25°C
Clarity - clear

Microbiological Tests Using Optimum Inoculum Dilution

Inoculate when cool and then aseptically seal with a layer of sterile 2% w/v Agar No.3 (LP0013)
solution.

A satisfactory result is represented by visible growth and gas.

Prepare 10ml volumes of medium in universal bottles. Sterilize and cool, then aseptically seal with
a layer of sterile 2% w/v Agar No.3 (LP0013) solution. Incubate at 55°C for 3 days. There shall be
no gas or discolouration of the medium in any tube.
