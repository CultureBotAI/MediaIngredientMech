474.     MEDIUM WITH POLYHYDROXYBUTYRIC ACID
         AS CARBON SOURCE

Prepare a suspension of 3.0 g poly-ß-hydroxybutyric acid (PHB) in 100 ml distilled water,
stir over night and sonicate until a white homogenous suspension is obtained. Autoclave
for 5 minutes at 121C. Prepare double layer agar plates. First layer: medium 457 with
16.0 g agar per l. Second layer: medium 457 with agar, supplemented with 6.6 ml
sterile PHB-suspension per 100 ml.
