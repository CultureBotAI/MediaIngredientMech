1828: MWC

Final pH: 7.0 - 8.0
Final volume: 1000 ml


1. Prepare 1000 ml of DSMZ 1828 medium. To 800 ml of destilled water add:
   TES (dry buffer)                                           0.115         g
   NaNO3 (42.5 g/l stock solution)                            2.000        ml
   MgSO4 x 7 H2O (36.97 g/l stock solution)                   1.000        ml
   CaCl2 x 2 H2O (36.76 g/l stock solution)                   1.000        ml
   K2HPO4 x 3 H2O (4.35 g/l stock solution)                   2.620        ml
   NaHCO3 (31.5 g/l stock solution)                           0.400        ml
   Na2SiO3 x 5 H2O (21.2 g/l stock solution)                  1.000        ml
   Combined trace element solution*                           1.000        ml

2. Make up to 1 litre with destilled water.
3. Autoclave at 121 °C for 20 min.
4. After cooling, add 1 ml of the filter sterilised Vitamin mix under sterile conditions.
   MWC Vitamin mix**                                          1.000        ml




Combined trace element solution*
1. Prepare 1000 ml of a combined trace element solution (10-fold concentrated).
   Na2-EDTA                                                   4.360         g
   FeCl3 x 6 H2O                                              3.150         g
   H3BO3                                                      1.000         g
   CuSO4 x 5 H2O (12.5 g/l stock solution)                    0.800        ml
   ZnSO4 x 7 H2O (22.0 g/l stock solution)                    1.000        ml
   CoCl2 x 6 H2O (6.0 g/l stock solution)                     1.667        ml
   MnCl2 x 4 H2O (18.0 g/l stock solution)                    1.000        ml
   Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O (2.7 g/l stock solution)                   2.222        ml

2. The pH of the combined trace metal solution is best adjusted to 4.0-4.5 (Guillard &
Lorenzen, 1972).
3. Make up to 1000 ml with distilled water.



MWC Vitamin mix**
1. Prepare 100 ml of the Vitamin mix from the following stock solutions (thawed from - 20
°C).
