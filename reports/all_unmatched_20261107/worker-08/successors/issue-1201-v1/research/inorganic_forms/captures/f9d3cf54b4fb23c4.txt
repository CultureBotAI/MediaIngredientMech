Prepare the liquid medium. Adjust to pH 6.2 with HCl or NaOH. Dispense the medium
into serum bottles under gassing with N2:CO2 (80:20 v/v) for one minute. 20% methane
and 5% air is added to the gas phase
Sterilize by autoclaving. Before inoculation add from sterile stock solutions:
Vitamin solution                                                     1.0    ml/l
NaHCO3 (5% w/v)                                                     50.0    ml/l
CuSO4 x 5 H2O (0.1 M)                                               10.0    ml/l
