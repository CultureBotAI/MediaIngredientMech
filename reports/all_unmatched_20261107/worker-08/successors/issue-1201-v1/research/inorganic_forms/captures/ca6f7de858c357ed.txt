1. Dissolve ingredients (except calcium chloride, bicarbonate, glucose, vitamins, cysteine
and sulfide), adjust pH to 7.0 and sparge medium with 100% N2 gas for 30 - 45 min to
make it anoxic. Dispense medium under same gas atmosphere into anoxic Hungate-type
tubes or serum vials and autoclave. After sterilization add calcium chloride, glucose,
cysteine and sulfide from sterile anoxic stock solutions autoclaved under 100% N2 gas
atmosphere. Add bicarbonate from a sterile anoxic stock solution prepared under 80% N2
and 20% CO2 gas mixture. Vitamins are prepared under 100% N2 gas and sterilized by
filtration. The pH of the complete medium should be at 7.0.
2. Note: Supplementation of medium with 10.00 g/l MOPS buffer (pH 6.9 - 7.0; 10% (w/v)
anoxic stock solution) may enhance the buffer capacity of the medium.
