Mix components thoroughly, except agar, and adjust pH to 7.0.  Add agarose to the medium and autoclave under a N2 gas atmosphere.  After cooling to 50C, add the following solutions to the medium aseptically and anaerobically (autoclaved or *filter-sterilized):    
 0.5 M Potassium phosphate buffer (pH 7.0)                 |  1.8     |  ml   | 
 40% Na2S2O3·5H2O solution              |  3.0     |  ml   | 
 Vitamin solution* (see below)                             |  0.5     |  ml   | 
 5% L-Cysteine·HCl·H2O solution           |  4.0     |  ml   | 
 8% NaHCO3 solution*                                   |  2.35    |  ml   | 
Keep the medium temperature at 50C until the medium is distributed into sterile screw-capped test tubes as described below.  
Adjust pH to 7.0 - 7.2 with sterile HCl or NaOH solution.  Then, aseptically and anaerobically, add the following solution (autoclaved):  
 0.01 M FeSO4·5H2Osolution (in 0.02 N HCl)     |  2.5     |  ml   | 
Dispense the medium to sterile screw-capped test tubes (typically 12 ml medium into 125 by 15 mm test tubes) under air and cap.  Allow to stand until the medium is solidified and a O2-gradient formed (as evidenced by a pink upper oxic zone and a colorless lower anoxic zone).  Inoculate cells just below the pink-colorless interface in the medium tube using a syringe.  
Modified Wolfe's mineral solution: 
Use trace minerals (see Medium No. 151) with final 0.4 g/L Na2MoO4·2H2O, 0.02 g/L CuSO4·5H2O and 0.01 g/L NiCl2·6H2O.
