  |   | This medium is for co-culture of strain JT with Methanospirillum hungatei strain NBRC 100397. | 
  |   | For vial cultures, dissolve all ingredients except for Na-terephthalate, Na2S·9H2O and cysteine-HCl (up to 900ml), flush N2/CO2 (80/20), and autoclave.  Add 0.5 ml of each of filter-sterilized Na2S·9H2O solution (0.6g Na2S·9H2O/50 ml) and cysteine-HCl solution (0.6g cysteine-HCl/ 50ml) per each vial separately.  Add 0.2 ml of neutralized and filter-sterilized 0.2 M Na-terephthalate solution. Yeast extract is also added at final concentration about 0.02%.  Inoculum size 10-20%.  Growth was observed after 3-4 weeks of incubation. |
