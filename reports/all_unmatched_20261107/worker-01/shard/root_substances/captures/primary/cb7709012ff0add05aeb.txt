673. THERMOCOCCUS PROFUNDUS MEDIUM


Prepare medium 266 with the following modification: reduce the yeast extract
concentration to 1 g/l, add 5 g/l peptone and reduce the NaCl concentration to 25 g/l.
Prepare the medium under N2 as described in medium 266. The final pH should be
adjusted to pH 7.2.
