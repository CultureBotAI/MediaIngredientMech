1279. HELICOBACTER MEDIUM


 Brucella Broth (Becton Dickinson)                               2.8      g
 Aqua dem.                                                      80.0     ml

Adjust with HCl to pH 5.0, make anoxic under a stream of N2/CO2, distribute in Hungate
tubes to 8 ml and autoclave.
Add aseptically to each tube:

 Fetal Bovine Serum (heat inactivated)                          2.00     ml
 Vitox (Oxoid)                                                  0.06     ml
 Skirrow supplement (Oxoid)                                     0.02     ml
 Amphotericin (10 mg/ml)                                        0.02     ml

Add 1 ml of sterile air to each tube.
