Ferric EDTA solution:
1) Dissolve 17.9 g sodium EDTA and 3.28 g NaOH in 186 ml of distilled water.
2) Dissolve 13.7 g FeSO4 x 7 H2O in 364 ml of distilled ater, bubbling with air overnight,
and store in a brown glass bottle.
3) Combine the two solutions and make up to 1 litre with distilled water.

Autoclave the mineral salts solution and add 10 ml/l filter sterilised methanol to the
cooled medium. Add 15 g/l agar for solid media.
