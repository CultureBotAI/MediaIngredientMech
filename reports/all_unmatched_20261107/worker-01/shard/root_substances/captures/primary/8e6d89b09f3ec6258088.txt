799. DESULFOVIBRIO INOPINATUS MEDIUM


Use medium 193 with 1 ml/l vitamin solution of medium 503 (sol. E), 1 ml/l
selenite-tungstate solution of medium 385, 5 ml/l 10% (w/v) yeast extract, and 10 ml/l
25% (w/v) Na-pyruvate or 2mM 1,2,4-trihydroxybenzene (from filter sterilized,
anaerobic stock solution) replacing acetate (sol. D).
