1189. AEROBIC SULFOLOBALES MEDIUM WITHOUT SULFUR

Solution A:
 Salt base solution (see below)                               1000.0       ml
 Yeast extract (Difco)                                           0.5        g

Autoclave salt base solution and yeast extract (as a 10% solution) separately, and
combine after cooling.

Salt base solution:
(NH4)2 SO4                                                      1.30       g
KH2PO4                                                          0.28       g
 MgSO4 x 7 H2O                                                  0.25       g
 CaCl2 x 2 H2O                                                  0.07       g
 MnCl2 x 4 H2O                                                  1.80      mg
 Na2B4O7 x 10 H2O                                               4.50      mg
 ZnSO4 x 7 H2O                                                  0.22       g
 CuCl2 x 2 H2O                                                  0.05      mg
 Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O                                                0.03      mg
 VOSO4 x n H2O                                                  0.03      mg
 CoSO4 x 7 H2O                                                  0.01      mg
 Distilled water                                             1000.00      ml

Adjust to pH 2.5 with 10 N H2SO4.
