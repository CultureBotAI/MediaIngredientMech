Adjust final pH to 5.8.
Prepare the medium anaerobically under 100% nitrogen. The following constituents are
prepared separately and added to the autoclaved mineral salt solution: Yeast extract (20
ml of 10% w/v solution)-boiled for a few minutes not autoclaved; sulfur (10 g)-sterilized
by steaming for 3 h on each of three successive days; Na2S x 9 H2O (10 ml of 3% w/v
solution)-autoclaved under nitrogen atmosphere.
