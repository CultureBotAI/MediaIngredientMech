Add solutions B and C to solution A, adjust pH of the combined solution to 1.6 - 1.8 with

For DSM 16651: Supplement medium prior to inoculation with 2.0 g/l potassium
tetrathionate added from a stock solution sterilized by filtration. Incubate without
agitation.
For DSM 18409: Replace trace elements with 1.00 ml/l of trace element solution SL-10 (see
medium 320). Supplement medium prior to inoculation with 1.50 g/l potassium
tetrathionate added from a stock solution sterilized by filtration.
