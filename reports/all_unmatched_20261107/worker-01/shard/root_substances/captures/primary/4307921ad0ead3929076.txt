                           BG11 (Blue-Green Medium)
                                  Freshwater algae and protozoa



Stocks                                                                  per 500ml
          (1) NaNO3                                                     75.0 g

                                                                        per 100 ml
          (2) K2HPO4                                                    4.0 g
          (3) MgSO4.7H2O                                                7.5 g
          (4) CaCl2.2H2O                                                3.6 g
          (5) Citric acid*                                              0.60 g
          (6) Ammonium ferric citrate green*                            0.60 g
          (7) Na2EDTA                                                   0.10 g
          (8) Na2CO3                                                    2.00 g

          (9) Trace elements:                                           per litre
              H3BO3                                                     2.86 g
              MnCl2.4H2O                                                1.81 g
              ZnSO4.7H2O                                                0.22 g
              Na2MoO4.2H2O                                              0.39 g
              CuSO4.5H2O                                                0.08 g
              Co(NO3)2.6H2O                                             0.05 g



Medium                                                                  per litre
          Stock solution 1                                              10.0 ml
          Stock solutions 2 - 9                                         1.0 ml each


Make up to 1 litre with deionised water. Adjust pH to 7.1 with 1M NaOH or HCl.
For agar, add 15.0 g per litre of Bacteriological Agar. Autoclave at 15 psi for 15 minutes.


*Due to precipitation, larger volumes require stocks 5 & 6 to be autoclaved separately or filter
sterilised and added to sterile medium in the airflow cabinet.


BG110
For Nitrogen fixing cyanobacteria, prepare standard BG11 while omitting Stock 1.


Reference
Allen, M. M.; Stanier, R. Y. (1968). Growth and Division of Some Unicellular Blue-green
Algae. Journal of General Microbiology, 51(2), 199–202 – adapted for CCAP
