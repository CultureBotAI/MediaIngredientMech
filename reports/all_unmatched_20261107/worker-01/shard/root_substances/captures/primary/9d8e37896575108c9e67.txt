Mix components except yeast extract, bovine serum albumin and Na2S·9H2O. Bring to a boil for 5-10 sec and flush with N2. Adjust pH to 7.6 at 80-90C. After cooling to room temperature, dispense the medium into serum bottles under a N2 atmosphere, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave. Separately autoclave yeast extract as a 10% solution and neutralized Na2S·9H2O as a 5% solution under a N2 atmosphere. Filter-sterilize bovine serum albumin as a 10% (w/v) solution under a N2 atmosphere. Before inoculation, aseptically and anaerobically add the yeast extract, bovine serum albumin and neutralized Na2S·9H2O solutions.
