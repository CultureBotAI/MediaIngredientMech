 SAG™ 471
 Description
 SAG 471 antifoam is for use in both aqueous and non-aqueous systems, imparting
 outstanding durability in the control of foam over time. Highly effective (even at low
 concentrations), it offers cost-efficient foam control for a variety of applications,
 including aerobic fermentation of antibiotics involving actinomycetales organisms,
 glycol-based coolants, heat exchange systems, adhesives, sealants, vaccines, and
 others. SAG 471 antifoam is self-dispersing in water.

 Key Features and Typical Benefits

    maximization of fermentor capacities
    excellent durability
    excellent foam control performance in fermentation processes
    self-dispersing in water
    suitability under sterilization
    autoclavable
