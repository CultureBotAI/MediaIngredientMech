Dissolve ingredients except carbonate, vitamins and sulfide, then sparge medium with
80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture for 30 - 45 min to make it anoxic. Dispense medium
under same gas atmosphere into anoxic Hungate-type tubes or serum vials and autoclave.
Prior to inoculation add vitamins and sulfide from sterile anoxic stock solutions prepared
under 100% N2 gas and carbonate from a sterile anoxic stock solution prepared under 80%
N2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture. Stock solutions of vitamins should be sterilized by filtration.
Adjust pH of complete medium to 7.0, if necessay.
