ATCC Medium 173: Middlebrook 7H10 Agar w/ OADC

Base Medium
Middlebrook 7H10 Agar…………………………………. 19.0 g
Glycerol……………………………………………………. 5.0 mL
Dubos Oleic Albumin Complex (OADC, see below)…. 100.0 mL
DI Water…………………………………………………… 900.0 mL

Add and mix all components except for OADC.

Bring to a boil to dissolve agar and autoclave at 121ºC.

Cool to 50-55ºC and aseptically add OADC.

OADC
Oleic Acid (Sigma Cat # O-1008)………………………….. 0.05 g
Albumin Fraction V Bovine (Sigma A9647)……….……… 5.0 g
Dextrose (BD 215530)…………………………………….... 2.0 g
Catalase (beef) (Sigma C-9322)…………………………... 0.004 g
NaCl…………………………………………………………... 0.85 g
DI Water……………………………………………………… 100.0 mL

Filter sterilize.

OADC is also available from BD (237510).
