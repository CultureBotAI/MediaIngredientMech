629. STALEY'S MAINTENANCE MEDIUM

Peptone                                                            5.0       g
Yeast extract                                                      0.5       g
Hutner's salts (see medium 590)                                   20.0      ml
Staley's vitamins (filter-sterilized)
(see medium 600)                                                 10.0       ml
Distilled water                                                 970.0       ml

Dissolve peptone and yeast extract in the distilled water, adjust the pH to 7.2 and
sterilize. After the medium has cooled add the Hutner's salts and Staley's vitamins.
For solid medium use 15.0 g agar.

Hutner's salts:
Nitrilotriacetic acid                                            10.0       g
MgSO4 x 7 H2O                                                    29.7       g
CaCl2 x 2 H2O                                                     3.3       g
NaMoO4 x 2 H2O                                                   12.7      mg
FeSO4 x 2 H2O                                                    99.0      mg
"Metals 44" (see medium 600)                                     50.0      ml
Distilled water                                                 950.0      ml
