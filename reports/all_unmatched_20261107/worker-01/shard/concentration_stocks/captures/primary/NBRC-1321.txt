pH 7.0-7.3
Mix ingredients except for yeast extract, bicarbonate, vitamin, thiamine, cyanocobalamin and sulfide solutions. Dispense the medium into suitable culture vessels under a stream of the N2/CO2 (80/20) mixed gas, seal with butyl rubber stoppers, and autoclave at 121°C for 15 min. Aseptically and anaerobically add other ingredients using sterile syringes. Adjust pH to 7.0-7.3 using compact pH meter.
