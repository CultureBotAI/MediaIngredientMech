Mix components thoroughly and adjust pH to 5.0 with HCl. Bring to a boil for 5 min, then, cool down under a stream of a N2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) gas mixture. Distribute the medium into Balch-type culture tubes (5 ml each) under the same gas mixture, seal with thick blue butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
To complete the medium, replace the gas phase with H2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) gas mixture, and add the following solutions to each of the culture tubes (containing 5 ml medium) from sterile anaerobic stocks (autoclaved or *filter-sterilized):
