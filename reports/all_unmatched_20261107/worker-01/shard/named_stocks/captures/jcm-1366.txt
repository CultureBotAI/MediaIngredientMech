1366 GBS SALTS MEDIUM FOR HYDROGENOBACTER
NaCl | 3.0 | g
KCl | 0.15 | g
Na2SO4 | 0.3 | g
MgSO4·7H2O | 0.123 | g
CaCl2·2H2O | 14.5 | mg
NH4Cl | 0.107 | g
Mineral solution A (see Medium No. 1353) | 5.0 | ml
Distilled water | 1.0 | L
Mix components thoroughly and distribute the medium into culture vessels (e.g., 5.0 ml in 25 ml serum bottles/Balch tubes) under a stream of N2, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave. After cooling, aseptically add per liter the following solutions (autoclaved or *filter-sterilized and stored anaerobically):
8% NaHCO3 solution* | 12.5 | ml
1.0 M Na2S2O3 solution | 2.5 | ml
0.5 M Na2HPO4 solution (pH 8.0) | 20.0 | ml
After inoculation, add air to the gas phase (30-40% by volume) and pressurize to 100 KPa with H2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas mixture.
