Mix components thoroughly, except sulfur, and autoclave under a N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas atmosphere. After cooling, add the following solutions from anaerobic stocks (autoclaved or *filter-sterilized):
8% NaHCO3 solution* | 30.0 | ml
Vitamin solution (see Medium No. 403)* | 1.0 | ml
Thiamine solution (see Medium No. 403)* | 1.0 | ml
Vitamin B12 solution (see Medium No. 403)* | 1.0 | ml
Steam sulfur for 3 hr on each of 3 successive days. Aseptically and anaerobically, distribute the medium into culture vessels containing appropriate amount of sulfur under the same gas mixture (e.g., 20 ml in 60 ml-serum bottles containing 0.05 g sulfur) and seal with butyl rubber stoppers.
