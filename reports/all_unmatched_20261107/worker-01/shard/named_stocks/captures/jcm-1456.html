Mix components, except NaHCO3, and bring to a boil for 5-10 seconds. Cool down under a stream of N2-CO2 (80:20 v/v), and add NaHCO3 to the medium. Adjust pH to 7.3 -7.6. Dispense the medium into culture vessels under a stream of H2-CO2 (80:20 v/v), seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave. Prior to inoculation, add per liter the following solutions (autoclaved and stored under a N2 atmosphere).
5% Na2S·9H2O solution   10.0     ml
5% L-Cysteine·HCl·H2O solution   10.0     ml
Pressurize inoculated vessels to 100 kPa with a H2-CO2 (80:20 v/v) gas mixture.
