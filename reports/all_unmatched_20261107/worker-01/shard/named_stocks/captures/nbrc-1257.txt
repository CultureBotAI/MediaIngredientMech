pH 6.8-7.0
Mix ingredients except for bicarbonate and sulfide solutions. Dispense the medium into suitable culture vessels under a stream of nitrogen gas, seal with butyl rubber stoppers, and autoclave at 121°C for 15 min. Aseptically and anaerobically add other ingredients using sterile syringes, and the N2/CO2 (80/20) mixed gas is enclosed with the vessels. Adjust pH to 6.8-7.0 using compact pH meter.
